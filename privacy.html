Data Fiduciary: This Privacy Policy outlines how TECHSTAR MONEY SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED (CIN: U66190MH2026PTC478675 | GSTIN: 27AANCT3833C1ZB), having its principal operating office at TECHSTAR MONEY SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED, Office No 18, Morya Pride, Mayur Park, Mhasoba Nagar, Harsul, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra 431001, India ("Techstar", "we", "us", or "our"), collects, processes, stores, and protects customer data across our website and SaaS application.

1. Scope & Commitment to Privacy

Techstar ERP is a specialized Indian multi-tenant cloud enterprise accounting and operations system. We recognize that our business customers entrust us with sensitive financial, accounting, and commercial transactional data.

We adhere strictly to the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, Information Technology Act, 2000, and standard international data protection principles. We never sell, monetize, or rent your business data to advertisers or third-party brokers.

2. Categories of Data We Collect

In order to provide our cloud ERP services, we process the following categories of information:

  • Account Identification Data: Full name, official email address, mobile number, and encrypted password hash (bcrypt work factor 12) collected upon registration.
  • Commercial & Business Profile: Company name, trade name, business classification (e.g. Retail, Wholesale, Manufacturing), GSTIN, PAN, registered office address, pincode, state, and branch designations.
  • Operational & ERP Transactional Data: Invoices, billing receipts, product inventory catalogs, FIFO stock logs, customer and supplier ledgers, chart of accounts, and financial journal entries created by your users.
  • Security & Audit Logs: Timestamps, user IDs, user roles, action types (e.g. login, payment status update, invoice creation), and branch associations logged in your private tenant security trail.
  • Attribution & Session Data: First-party browser storage (for a campaign visit, up to 90 days; see section 5), one-time demo handoff exchange codes, and standard UTM campaign parameters (source, medium, campaign) and, if you arrive from a Meta (Facebook / Instagram) advertisement, the ad-click identifier (fbclid), captured to evaluate marketing effectiveness.
  • Trial and plan messages: During and just after a free trial we send the account's e-mail address and, once the templates are approved, its WhatsApp number a few service messages: a welcome, getting-started tips, that the trial ends soon (or that an AutoPay debit is due), and that it has ended. They come from Techstar ERP only and stop once a plan is chosen or a few days after the trial ends.
  • Website Enquiries: When you book a demo or contact us on this website we collect your name, mobile number, e-mail (optional), business name, business type, city, preferred demo time and your message, together with the page and campaign you came from. We use them to contact you about Techstar ERP and, with the mobile number and e-mail in hashed form only, to measure our advertising (section 5), and keep them for up to 24 months after our last contact, or until you ask us to delete them.

3. Payment Processing & Cashfree Payments Disclosure

All subscription payments and billing transactions are executed through our authorized payment processing partner, Cashfree Payments India Private Limited ("Cashfree"):

  • No Storage of Sensitive Card Credentials: All credit card numbers, debit card numbers, CVVs, expiration dates, Net Banking credentials, and UPI PINs are processed directly by Cashfree within their PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant secure vault. Techstar does not store or have access to your card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Data Exchanged with Cashfree: We share only the minimum required metadata with Cashfree to generate payment sessions and invoices: Customer Name, Email, Phone Number, Order ID, and Subscription Amount.
  • Webhook Verification: Payment confirmations are verified server-side using cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signatures with replay timestamp protection.

4. Multi-Tenant Architectural Isolation & Security

Techstar ERP implements rigorous database isolation:

  • Tenant Separation: Data belonging to your business workspace is partitioned using unique company identifiers and enforced through server-side authorization checks and database security rules. No other customer can access your ledgers or reports.
  • Encryption Standards: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Data stored in our database is encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Where your data is stored: ERP data is kept in a managed PostgreSQL database (Supabase) in the Mumbai, India region. Owners and Admins can download a complete backup of their company data at any time.
  • Service providers (sub-processors): Vercel (application hosting), Supabase (database), Google Firebase (sign-in and this website's hosting), Cashfree Payments (subscription payments), Meta Platforms (advertising measurement through the Meta Pixel on this website, see section 5, and WhatsApp service messages), Google (website visit statistics through Google Analytics on this website, see section 5) and our e-mail delivery provider. If you later connect a GST Suvidha Provider (for e-Invoice / e-Way Bill) or your own Google Drive (for backups), the data you send there is shared with that service only on your instruction.
  • Business loan referral (only with your separate consent): TECHSTAR MONEY SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED also works as a loan referral partner (DSA) for banks and NBFCs. Only if you separately opt in, we may use your company's own ERP records — such as sales, purchases, receivables and GST return summaries — to check your eligibility for business loans and share them with the lender you choose. This is never a condition for using Techstar ERP, you can withdraw your consent at any time by e-mailing the Grievance Officer below, and we do not use the personal data of your customers, suppliers or staff for this purpose.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential functional cookies and local browser storage necessary for application functionality, and, on this website, Google Analytics to count visits and the Meta Pixel to measure our advertising, both described below:

  • Authentication Session: Secure tokens to maintain your login status across ERP modules without requiring repeated logins.
  • Campaign Attribution: When you arrive on this website from one of our advertisements or campaign links, your browser's local storage keeps the campaign name and the ad-click identifier for up to 90 days (a visit without a campaign is kept only until you close the tab), so that a demo booking or a trial you start later is credited to that campaign. It stays in your browser and is sent to us only with a demo booking, a contact message or a trial sign-up.
  • Google Analytics (visit statistics): This website uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google LLC, to count visits and see which pages and buttons are used (for example "Start free trial" or "Book a demo"), so that we can improve the website. Google sets cookies such as _ga and receives the pages you view, your browser and device details, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and those button events. We do not send what you type into our forms to Google. Google handles this information under its own Privacy Policy. You can stop it by blocking cookies or with Google's opt-out add-on. The ERP application itself loads no analytics script.
  • Meta Pixel (advertising measurement): This website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. and Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, to measure the results of our advertisements on Facebook and Instagram and to show our advertisements to people who may be interested in Techstar ERP. When you visit this website, Meta sets cookies in your browser (such as _fbp and _fbc) and receives the pages you view, your browser and device details, your IP address, and the actions you take: booking a demo, sending us a contact message, or tapping the WhatsApp or Call button. We do not send what you type into our forms (name, mobile number, e-mail, business name or message) to Meta through the Pixel; our server sends only your mobile number and e-mail in hashed form, as described in the next point. Meta handles the information it receives under its own Privacy Policy. You can stop this by blocking cookies in your browser, by using a tracker-blocking extension, or by changing your ad settings in your Facebook / Instagram account (Settings → Ad preferences).
  • Enquiry, sign-up and purchase events (Meta Conversions API): To measure our advertising, and so that Meta can learn which enquiries become customers, our server tells Meta when you book a demo or send us a contact message on this website, when a free trial starts, when a company makes its first invoice during the trial (only that it happened, never the invoice), and when a company's first paid plan is activated (with the plan and the amount excluding GST). With each event we send the mobile number and e-mail address you gave us in hashed (one-way encrypted) form, the ad-click identifier if you came from a Meta advertisement, the Meta cookies this website set in your browser (_fbp, _fbc; this website passes them to the ERP when you start a trial) and, for enquiries and the trial, the IP address and browser type used at that time. We never send your name, business name or message, nor invoices, ledgers, customer or supplier details or any other business records. The ERP application itself loads no advertising script.
  • No Other Advertising Trackers: We do not use any other third-party advertising or retargeting pixel (such as TikTok or LinkedIn scripts), and we never sell your data to advertisers.

6. Data Retention & Customer Deletion Rights

You maintain complete sovereignty over your data:

  • Active Subscription: Data is retained continuously throughout your active subscription.
  • Expired / Cancelled Accounts: Data remains archived in read-only format for a minimum of 90 days post-expiration, enabling you to export ledgers, invoices, and GST registers.
  • Right to Deletion: Workspace Owners may request permanent erasure of their company database by emailing official@swapnilaher.in. Erasure will be fulfilled within 30 days, subject to mandatory statutory tax retention requirements under Indian accounting and GST law.

7. Your Rights (DPDP Act, 2023)

  • You may ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you, and for its correction, completion or erasure.
  • You may withdraw your consent at any time by e-mailing the Grievance Officer below; we will then stop processing that data, except where the law requires us to keep it (for example tax records).
  • We reply to grievances within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our reply, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
