1. Nature of the Service & Digital Delivery

Techstar ERP is a cloud-hosted, multi-tenant Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application providing enterprise business management tools including double-entry accounting, GST invoice generation, POS counter terminals, real-time multi-branch inventory, and financial reporting.

Because Techstar ERP is exclusively a cloud-hosted software platform, all subscriptions are delivered instantly and electronically upon account creation and payment confirmation. There are no physical goods or physical shipments involved in the fulfillment of our services.

2. Free Trial Period (7 Days)

We encourage all businesses to thoroughly evaluate Techstar ERP prior to committing to a paid subscription:

  • Every newly registered business workspace receives a 7-day complimentary free trial of the Starter or Business plan.
  • No credit card or upfront payment is required to activate the 7-day trial.
  • If you decide not to purchase a paid subscription before the 7-day trial concludes, your account automatically transitions to an inactive (read-only) state without any automated billing or liability, unless you chose to set up AutoPay during the trial as described below.
  • AutoPay during the trial (optional): you may set up UPI AutoPay or a card mandate during the trial so that your plan continues without a break. Your bank or UPI app takes ₹1 to verify the mandate, which is refunded; nothing is charged for the trial. The first full debit of the plan you chose is made after the trial ends (at about 4:30 AM India time); its date and amount are shown before you authorize, and your bank notifies you at least 24 hours before it. Cancel AutoPay any time before that debit and nothing is charged; the trial still runs to its end.

4. Role of Cashfree Payments (Payment Aggregator)

All subscription checkouts, one-time payments, and recurring AutoPay mandates are processed securely through our authorized payment processing infrastructure partner, Cashfree Payments India Private Limited ("Cashfree").

  • Cashfree acts solely as an independent, RBI-authorized Payment Aggregator / Payment Gateway infrastructure provider. Cashfree does not manage or operate the Techstar ERP application.
  • All sensitive payment credential processing (debit cards, credit cards, Net Banking, UPI, and e-mandates) occurs strictly within Cashfree's PCI-DSS compliant secure vault.
  • TECHSTAR MONEY SOLUTION PRIVATE LIMITED does NOT store card numbers, CVV codes, expiry dates, or UPI PINs on any of our servers.

5. Cancellation Policy

Customers have complete control over their subscriptions and may cancel recurring mandates at any time:

  • Self-Service Cancellation: Company Owners and Admins can cancel their active plan directly from the authenticated ERP portal under https://erp.techstarsolution.in/dashboard/subscription by clicking "Cancel Subscription".
  • Cancellation Notice Period: Cancellation takes effect at the end of the currently paid billing cycle (e.g. at the end of the 30-day month or 365-day year).
  • Continued Operational Access: Following cancellation, your workspace retains full read and write access until the expiration date of the existing paid term. No subsequent recurring charges will be initiated.
  • Data Retention Post-Cancellation: Your business records (invoices, ledgers, inventory, reports) remain accessible in read-only mode for a minimum of 90 days following expiration, allowing you to export your data in Excel/PDF formats.

6. Failed Payments & 3-Day Grace Period

If an automatic recurring subscription renewal fails (e.g., due to insufficient bank balance, expired card, or banking server timeout), Techstar ERP provides an operational 3-day grace period. During these 3 days, your workspace remains fully operational while our payment system retries the charge or notifies you to update payment details. If payment is not completed after 3 days, write access is temporarily paused until renewal.

7. Refund Policy & Eligibility

As a digitally provisioned enterprise software platform with a zero-risk 7-day trial period, the following refund terms apply:

Eligible for Full (100%) Refund:

  • Duplicate / Accidental Deductions: If a technical error causes your bank or UPI account to be debited more than once for the same billing cycle or order ID, you are entitled to a 100% refund of the duplicate amount.
  • Billing Discrepancy / Unauthorized Transaction: Any verified billing discrepancy reported to our support team within 7 calendar days of transaction.
  • Non-Provisioning: In the rare event that payment was confirmed by Cashfree but your ERP workspace failed to activate within 24 hours due to an unrecoverable system defect.

Non-Refundable Circumstances:

  • Voluntary Early Cancellation: Because full digital license access is immediately granted, pro-rated refunds for partial months or unused portions of an annual term are not provided. You will retain operational access until the paid period ends.
  • Failure to Cancel Prior to Renewal: Automated renewals that were not cancelled prior to the scheduled billing date are non-refundable for the current active period, but can be cancelled for all future periods.
  • Account Suspension for Violation: Accounts terminated or suspended due to fraud, unlawful activities, or violation of our Terms of Service are not eligible for refunds.

8. How to Request a Refund & Processing Timelines

To request a refund for an eligible duplicate or erroneous payment:

  1. Send an email to official@swapnilaher.in within 7 days of the transaction.
  2. Include your registered Company Name, Account Email, Cashfree Order ID / Payment ID (from your receipt), and a screenshot or reference of the deduction.
  3. Our billing team will review and verify the gateway logs with Cashfree within 2 business days.
  4. Once approved, the refund is initiated automatically via Cashfree API back to the original source payment method (bank account, credit/debit card, or UPI VPA).
  5. Refunds typically credit to your account within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank's processing cycle.
