How to return goods to a supplier and make a debit note

Two ways to reduce what you owe a supplier. Return goods on the bill when goods physically go back — stock goes down too. Make a debit note for a rate difference, a discount after purchase or damaged goods kept on value — only the amounts change.

⏱ About 3 minutes Menu: Purchases → Debit notes Returns: ⋯ → Return goods on the bill

Before you start

ℹ️

The supplier's bill must already be entered (see How to add a purchase bill). A return and a GST debit note are both made against that bill.

Which one to use:

  • Return goods — the goods go back to the supplier. Stock goes down, and the bill's balance, the supplier's balance and the GST credit are reduced.
  • Debit note — no goods move: a rate difference, a discount after purchase, damaged or rejected goods settled on value. Only the amounts (and, with GST, the GST credit) change.

Return goods on the bill

Open Purchases → Purchase bills, click ⋯ on the bill's row A and choose Return goods B. The option is not shown once all the goods of a bill have been returned.

Enter the quantities and save the return

The Return goods to supplier window shows the supplier's bill number, our bill number and the supplier. For each item you see the billed quantity, the quantity returned before and the quantity that Can return.

  • Return qty A — starts at the full quantity that can be returned. Enter what you are sending back, and 0 for the items you keep.
  • The box below B shows the goods returned (without GST), the GST reversed and how much less you owe the supplier — an estimate; the exact figures are worked out when you save. Under a reverse-charge bill only the goods come off what you owe.

Click Save return C. The message says Return saved. Stock and the supplier's balance are updated. The bill now shows Part returned (or Returned when everything went back).

Open Debit notes

In the left menu click Purchases → Debit notes A. The screen lists the debit notes with the bill each one is against, the reason, the GST reversed and the total debited. Below them, Purchase returns (goods sent back) lists the returns made with Return goods C, numbered PR-….

Click + New debit note B.

Choose the supplier, the bill and the kind of note

  • Supplier — type the name, GSTIN or mobile and pick the supplier.
  • Supplier's bill A — the bill being debited (the supplier's latest bills are offered). The GST rates of the lines come from it.
  • Kind of note B — With GST (goods returned / rate difference) reduces the GST credit taken on the bill; Without GST (plain discount) changes no GST credit and is not in the GST returns. A note with GST must name a bill.
  • The grey box C shows the bill total, what was already returned or debited, and what is Left to debit. A note larger than that cannot be saved.
  • Lines — a description, the GST %, and the Value without GST (₹) D; the GST is worked out. Use + Add line for a second GST rate.
💡

One note for several bills. After choosing a bill, + Add another bill of this supplier adds more bills of the same supplier; Split the debit oldest first shares the note over them, or type each bill's share.

Choose the reason and settlement, then save

  • Reason A — Rate difference, Discount after purchase, Damaged goods, Rejected for quality or Goods returned.
  • Settled by B — Adjust against the supplier's account (you owe less), or Refund received in bank / in cash when the supplier pays you back.
  • Remarks — why the supplier is debited.

The totals C show the value without GST, the CGST + SGST (or IGST) reversed and You owe the supplier less by. Click Save debit note D.

After saving

The message Debit note DN-… saved. You owe the supplier less. appears and the note is at the top of the list A with the bill it is against, the reason, the GST reversed and the total debited. In the books it credits Purchases and the input GST of the bill and debits the supplier (or cash / bank for a refund); a note without GST credits Discount Received.

⚠️

Cancelling a note. Open the note (eye icon) → Cancel note and give the reason. Its journal is reversed on its own date, and the bill's balance, the supplier's account and the GST credit are restored. The owner, an admin, an accountant or a branch manager can cancel. It is refused when the note's month is closed in the books, or when the GST return of that month has already been filed — then correct it with a new note in an open month.

Common questions

I chose “Goods returned” as the reason of a debit note. Does stock go down?
No. A debit note changes only amounts. When goods physically go back, use Return goods on the bill (step 1) so that the stock goes down as well.
The supplier gave a discount after the bill, with no GST on it.
Make a debit note with Without GST (plain discount) and the reason Discount after purchase. No GST credit changes and it is not in the GST returns; the bill can be left blank.
The bill was already paid. Can I still return goods?
Yes. The supplier's balance then goes below zero: the supplier owes you. When you next open Pay supplier for them (see How to pay a supplier), the supplier box shows it as Advance with supplier.
Where do debit notes appear for GST?
A note with GST reduces the GST credit of the bill and is listed in your GST registers. Notes without GST are not in the GST returns.
