How to add a supplier (GSTIN, terms and bank account)

Add each supplier once with the GSTIN, State, contact details, payment terms and bank account. The ERP checks the GSTIN as you type, can fetch the name and address for you, and uses the State to decide CGST + SGST or IGST on the supplier's bills.

⏱ About 2 minutes Menu: Purchases → Suppliers Shortcut: Ctrl+S saves

Before you start

ℹ️

Keep the supplier's details at hand: GSTIN, mobile number, address and, if you pay them by bank transfer, their bank account and IFSC code. A bill or letterhead from the supplier usually has all of it.

Who can do it: the owner, an admin, a branch manager or a purchase manager can add and change suppliers. An opening balance can be entered only by the owner, an admin, an accountant or a branch manager.

Open the supplier list

In the left menu click Purchases → Suppliers A, then + Add supplier B.

The list shows each supplier's GSTIN and PAN, State, mobile number, payment terms, balance side and balance. The boxes at the top add up what you owe suppliers (We owe them) and what parties owe you (They owe us). Search by name, GSTIN, mobile or city.

Enter the GSTIN, then check the GST status and State

The New supplier window has three steps, shown as tabs at the top: Details, Balance & terms and Bank account.

  • GSTIN A — type the 15-character GSTIN. The ERP checks it as you type: GSTIN looks valid and the State appear under the box, and the PAN is filled from the GSTIN.
  • Look up B — fetches the trade name, legal name and address from the GST taxpayer search (read-only). A GSTIN look-up is charged to your wallet at the listed rate. If nothing is found, type the details yourself.
  • GST status C — decides whether GST credit can be claimed on this supplier's bills (see the table).
  • State D — taken from the first two digits of the GSTIN. For a supplier without a GSTIN, choose it yourself. It decides whether the supplier's bills carry CGST + SGST or IGST.
GST statusUse it for
Registered (regular)A normal GST-registered supplier. The GSTIN is required.
Composition dealer (no GST credit)A supplier under the composition scheme. No GST credit on their bills.
UnregisteredA supplier without a GSTIN. Chosen for you when the GSTIN box is left empty.
Special Economic Zone (SEZ)A supplier in a Special Economic Zone.
Overseas (imports)A supplier outside India, for imports.

Tick You pay the GST on their bills (reverse charge) for a supplier whose GST you pay to the government yourself, for example a goods transport agency (GTA) or an advocate.

Enter the name, contact and address

  • Supplier name — the name used on bills (required). The look-up fills in the trade name; Trade name and Legal name are kept separately.
  • PAN — filled from the GSTIN. A PAN that does not match the GSTIN is not accepted.
  • Mobile A — required: a 10-digit Indian mobile number starting with 6, 7, 8 or 9. Add Contact person, Other mobile and Email if you have them.
  • Address B, City and Pincode — filled by the look-up; the PIN code has 6 digits.
  • Notes — for your own team only.

Click Next or the Balance & terms tab.

Choose the balance side, payment terms and opening balance

  • Balance side A — We owe them for a supplier you buy from (Sundry Creditors); They owe us for a party who owes you money, for example one you also sell to on credit (Sundry Debtors); or Other.
  • Payment terms B — Pay on delivery, Paid in advance or Due in 7, 15, 30, 45, 60 or 90 days. They are shown as the supplier's usual terms when you enter a purchase bill, order or goods received.
  • Credit limit (₹) — the credit the supplier allows you, if any.
  • Opening balance (₹) C — what you owed this supplier on the day you started with the ERP. It goes into the opening entry of your books. Leave 0 for a new supplier.
  • Balance now (₹) — read only. It changes only through purchase bills, payments and debit notes.
  • Active — can be used on purchases — untick it to stop using a supplier without deleting it.

Add the bank account and save

Open the Bank account tab A at the top of the window. This is the account you pay the supplier into by NEFT / RTGS:

  • Bank name, Account number and IFSC code B — the IFSC has 11 characters, for example SBIN0001234. The bank details are saved only when the bank name is filled.
  • Account type — current or savings account. Bank branch is optional.

Click Save supplier C or press Ctrl + S. If something is wrong, the field is marked in red, the message Please correct the marked fields appears and the step with that field opens.

💡

Use the tabs at the top of the window to move between Details, Balance & terms and Bank account in any order. The step you are on is shown at the bottom, for example Step 3 of 3 · Bank account.

After saving

The message Supplier saved. appears and the supplier is added to the list A with its GSTIN, PAN, State code, payment terms and a balance of ₹0.00. Each row has three actions:

  • the book icon — the supplier's ledger statement;
  • the pencil icon — edit the supplier (or click the name);
  • ⋯ → Delete supplier.

The supplier can now be chosen on purchase bills (see How to add a purchase bill), purchase orders and goods received (see Purchase order and goods received) and supplier payments (see How to pay a supplier). Next to those forms the ERP shows the supplier's GSTIN, type, State, phone, usual terms and balance payable.

⚠️

Deleting a supplier. A supplier with a balance, an opening balance, or any purchase bills, payments or debit notes cannot be deleted — the books keep the party's name, PAN and GSTIN. Untick Active instead. A saved GSTIN can be corrected, but not left blank.

Common questions

The supplier has no GSTIN. What do I do?
Leave the GSTIN box empty: the GST status changes to Unregistered. Choose the supplier's State yourself — it is required. No GST credit is taken on an unregistered supplier's bills.
Why does the State matter?
It decides the tax type on the supplier's bills: the same State as your branch means CGST + SGST, another State means IGST. With a GSTIN the State comes from its first two digits.
Look up did not find the supplier.
Type the supplier name, legal name and address yourself and save. The GSTIN is still checked for a mistyped character (the last character is a check digit) before the supplier is saved.
Where do I see what I owe a supplier?
In the Balance (₹) column of the list, in the supplier panel of every purchase form, and in the supplier's ledger statement (the book icon on the row). The Supplier statements tab on the Purchases screen shows all bills and payments of one supplier.
I also sell to this party on credit.
Choose They owe us as the balance side if the party mostly owes you money. The two tabs above the list, We owe them and They owe us, show each group separately.
