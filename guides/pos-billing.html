How to bill at the counter (POS)

The POS screen is made for quick counter sales. Scan the barcodes or search the items, take the payment with one key, and the receipt prints by itself. The ERP works out the GST from your prices, numbers the bill and reduces the stock when the bill is saved.

⏱ Under a minute per bill Menu: Sales → POS billing Shortcut: F8 opens it from any screen

Before you start

ℹ️

Your items are in the item master with a selling rate (Items & stock → Items); a barcode on the item lets you scan it. On the counter the selling rate is the price including GST, like an MRP; the ERP takes the GST out of it.

Counters and shifts are optional. If your shop has them (Sales → Counters, shifts & day-end), choose this computer's counter at the top of the screen and open its shift with the opening cash.

Open POS billing

In the left menu click Sales → POS billing A, or press F8 on any screen. On the counter the menu shrinks to icons to give the bill more room; point at an icon to see its screens.

The top bar shows the cashier, the keys and the next bill number B. The number is final only when the bill is saved, so the series has no gaps. Next to the scan box, Catalog C opens the item search, and the green badge (for example 6 items ready) means the items and stock are kept on this computer, so scanning does not wait for the internet.

Scan or search the items

Scan the barcode — the scanner works from anywhere on the screen, the scan box Scan or type a barcode… picks it up. Each scan adds one piece; scanning the same item again adds one more. To add several pieces in one go, type the quantity, a star and the barcode, for example 4*89000000003 for 4 pieces.

No barcode? Press F4 or click Catalog, type at least two letters of the name A, then pick the item with the arrow keys and Enter or a click B. The list shows the barcode, the stock and the rate.

💡

On a phone or tablet, Camera scans barcodes with the camera (where the browser supports it), and Touch shows big item tiles you can tap.

Check quantities and rates

Every item is a line in the cart, with its barcode, GST rate and the stock in hand. After each item the cursor goes back to the scan box A, ready for the next one.

  • Quantity B: click − or +, press ← / →, or type it. Part quantities such as 0.5 kg are allowed.
  • Rate C: the selling rate including GST. You can change it for this bill.
  • Remove a line with the bin icon or Del. Move between lines with ↑ / ↓.

The strip at the bottom D shows the GST of the bill by rate: CGST + SGST for a sale in your state, or IGST with an Inter-State badge.

⚠️

A red Short by badge means the cart has more than the stock at this branch. The server checks the stock again when the bill is saved.

Customer (optional)

For a walk-in customer you can leave Customer empty. To put a customer on the bill, type the name, mobile number or member code and press Enter. Customers who buy on credit carry the badge Credit (udhar) A with their dues and credit limit.

For a credit customer a box B checks the limit: Credit OK in green, or in red when the customer's credit is blocked, the limit is ₹0, or this bill would take the dues over the limit. The Credit button C works only when the check is green.

  • New customer: click New customer, enter Full name, Mobile and, if they have one, the GSTIN. Tick Gets credit (udhar) to give credit, then set the Credit limit (₹) — ₹0 means no credit. Click Save.
  • Bill to a business: type the buyer's GSTIN in GSTIN (optional). The tax then follows the state in the GSTIN.
  • Goods delivered to another state: for a buyer without GSTIN, change Supply from At counter to Deliver to … that state. The bill then has IGST.

Discount, cash and change

The right side shows the amount to collect, Net payable A, and how it is made up.

  • Discount B: press F7, choose ₹ or % and type it. The discount is shared over the lines before the GST is worked out, so the GST goes down with it.
  • Cash and change C: type what the customer hands over in Cash received (₹); Change (₹) shows what to give back.
  • A coupon or loyalty points can be entered under the discount and checked with Apply.

The big Cash button D (F8) saves the bill as a cash sale — see the next step for the other ways to pay.

Take the payment

One key saves the bill with its way of payment: F8 Cash, F9 UPI, F11 Card or F10 Credit (credit customers only). You can also click the buttons.

Or press End (Take payment) to see the total and choose: Cash A, UPI / bank, Card or Credit (udhar). Back (Esc) returns to the bill.

ℹ️

Cashier logins: a changed rate, a discount above 10% or a credit bill over the limit needs a manager. The cashier sees Manager approval needed and clicks Send for approval; the bill waits in Approvals until a manager approves it.

Big bill without GSTIN: for ₹50,000 or more (taxable value) the ERP asks for the buyer's name and address, which the bill must show.

Receipt, WhatsApp and the next bill

The bill is saved with its number, and the 80 mm receipt prints at once. The Bill saved window shows the total, the GST and, if you entered the cash received, the change to give.

  • Print receipt A prints it again.
  • Type the customer's mobile and click Send on WhatsApp B to send the PDF of the bill.
  • New bill C clears the counter for the next customer.
  • No printer at this counter? Untick Print the receipt automatically after every bill (this counter).

When a bill is saved, the stock goes down, the sale goes into your books with its GST and — for a credit bill — the customer's dues go up. If the goods need an e-Way Bill, the ERP shows an e-Way Bill window instead of Bill saved; see How to generate an e-Way Bill.

Hold a bill and recall it

Customer gone to fetch one more item? Press F3 or click Hold. The bill is put aside and the counter is free for the next customer. A Held bills button with the count appears at the top right.

Click Held bills and Recall A to bring the bill back with its items, customer and discount. Held bills are kept on the server, so any counter of the branch can recall them.

Common questions

Do the counter prices include GST?
Yes. On the POS screen the rate is the price the customer pays, GST included. The ERP takes the CGST and SGST (or IGST) out of it and shows them on the right and in the strip under the cart. For a tax invoice with rates before GST, use Sales → GST invoice.
Why is the Credit button greyed out?
Credit works only for a customer marked Credit (udhar), whose credit is active and who stays within the credit limit with this bill. Choose such a customer first; the green or red box above the buttons tells you why credit is not possible.
What happens if the internet goes off during a bill?
A cash, UPI or card bill can still be completed: the ERP prints a provisional slip (not a tax invoice) and books the bill by itself when the connection is back; then print the tax invoice for the customer. Only one such bill can wait at a time. A credit bill needs the internet, because the customer's limit is checked on the server.
How do I reprint, return or cancel a counter bill?
Counter bills are listed with all other bills in Sales → Invoices — see How to find, reprint or cancel an invoice. Goods coming back are handled as a sales return there.
Where do I add credit customers and collect their dues?
In Sales → Customers — see Customers and credit (udhar). You can also add a customer from the counter with New customer.
