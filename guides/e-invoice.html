How to generate an e-Invoice (IRN)

Get the IRN and the signed QR code for your invoices to registered buyers straight from Techstar ERP. The invoice is read from your books and sent to the e-invoice portal (IRP); the IRN, acknowledgement and QR code come back and are saved on the invoice, ready to print.

⏱ About 1 minute Menu: GST → E-Invoice / E-Way Bill One-time setup: e-Invoice API user

Before you start

ℹ️

Only invoices to registered buyers (customers with a GSTIN) are listed for e-invoicing. Cancelled invoices are left out.

e-Invoicing applies to you once your turnover is above ₹5 crore in any year since 2017-18, as the ERP's own hint says. If it does not apply to you, you do not need this guide.

Wallet: each IRN uses a usage charge from your wallet (Settings → Wallet & usage charges). If the portal refuses the invoice, the charge is returned; if the balance is too low, nothing is sent and the ERP asks you to recharge.

One-time setup: your e-Invoice portal login

  1. On einvoice1.gst.gov.in, create an API user for your GSTIN (API Registration → Create API user, choosing your GSP).
  2. In Techstar ERP open GST → E-Invoice / E-Way Bill A and the GST Configuration tab B.
  3. Pick your GSTIN on the left. Under e-Invoice portal (API user) C enter the Username and Password and click Verify & Save. The login is checked with the portal before it is saved, and the badge turns to Set up.
💡

Passwords are stored encrypted and never shown again. To keep a saved password, leave the box blank when you save. A login can be saved only for the GSTINs of your own company and branches (Settings → Business details & GST).

Say from when e-invoicing applies

Open the e-Invoices (IRN) tab A. In the card When e-invoicing applies B:

  • Applies from — the date from which e-invoicing applies to your business.
  • Tick Turnover ₹10 crore or more if that is your case: the e-invoice portal (IRP) then accepts an invoice only within 30 days of its date. The ERP stops an older invoice before it is sent.
  • Click Save.

While e-invoicing applies, a B2B invoice or credit note without its IRN prints only as a draft and cannot be e-mailed.

The card IRN for a credit note C is for a GST credit note to a registered buyer: choose Credit note or Sales return, type its number and click Generate IRN.

Open the e-Invoices list

Below the cards, e-Invoices (IRN) for B2B invoices lists your invoices to registered buyers with the customer, GSTIN, invoice total, IRN and e-Way Bill. A new invoice shows IRN pending B.

Use the tabs All invoices / Generated / Pending / Cancelled / Failed — for example Pending A — or search by invoice number, customer, GSTIN or IRN.

Generate the IRN

Click Generate IRN C on the invoice. The ERP builds the e-invoice from the invoice in your books, checks it, sends it to the IRP and saves the answer. When it is done you see e-Invoice generated.

The row now shows IRN Generated, the IRN and the acknowledgement number and time A.

⚠️

Generation failed? The portal’s reason is shown in a message, and the row shows Generation failed with a Retry button. Correct the invoice or the customer details if needed and click Retry — a retry is safe, because the IRP returns the existing IRN if there is one.

See the IRN and the QR code

Click the eye button B on the row. e-Invoice details shows what the IRP returned:

  • IRN (64 characters) A, with Copy;
  • Ack number, Ack date and Status, and the linked e-Way Bill if there is one;
  • the QR code from the IRP B, which is printed on the invoice.

Click Print e-Invoice (with QR) C to print the invoice with its IRN and QR code.

More actions on an invoice

The ⋯ button on each row lists what you can do with that invoice. For an invoice still pending:

  • Generate e-Invoice (IRN) and View invoice (amounts and items);
  • Download JSON for the IRP — the e-invoice file made from your books, if you want to upload it on the IRP portal yourself (nothing is sent from the ERP);
  • Generate e-Way Bill (without IRN) and Print invoice.

Once the IRN is generated, the menu offers View e-Invoice details, Copy IRN, Print e-Invoice (with QR), Generate e-Way Bill (from IRN) and Cancel e-Invoice. For the e-Way Bill itself, see How to generate an e-Way Bill.

Cancel an e-Invoice within 24 hours

Choose ⋯ → Cancel e-Invoice. The window reminds you of the Cancellation window A: an e-Invoice can be cancelled on the IRP only within 24 hours of generation, a cancellation cannot be undone, and the same invoice number cannot get a new IRN.

  • Reason B — 1 Duplicate, 2 Data Entry Mistake, 3 Order Cancelled or 4 Other.
  • Remarks C — required: why it is cancelled.

Click Cancel e-Invoice. The row then shows IRN Cancelled.

⚠️

An active e-Way Bill is linked? Cancel that e-Way Bill on the e-Way Bills tab first, then the IRN.

More than 24 hours have passed? The IRN cannot be cancelled any more; issue a credit note instead.

Other ways to generate the e-Invoice

  • Right after saving a sales invoice — for a registered buyer the ERP asks Generate the e-Invoice now? Click Generate e-Invoice (IRN) or Skip e-Invoice. If you skip it, the saved-invoice screen still has a Generate e-Invoice (IRN) button. See How to make a sales invoice.
  • Invoices tab — search for an invoice and click Generate IRN on its row.

Common questions

Why is my invoice not in the e-Invoices list?
The list shows only invoices to customers with a GSTIN, and not cancelled invoices. A walk-in (B2C) invoice does not get an IRN. The tab shows your latest 500 invoices; find an older one with the search on the Invoices tab.
I get an error about the login or credentials.
Save the e-Invoice API user under GST → E-Invoice / E-Way Bill → GST Configuration. The user name and password must be exactly as created on the e-invoice portal, and the login must be for the GSTIN of the branch that made the invoice.
The invoice is older than 30 days and the ERP refuses it.
That happens when you ticked Turnover ₹10 crore or more: the IRP then accepts an invoice only within 30 days of its date, so the ERP stops the request before it is sent.
Can I correct an invoice after the IRN is generated?
Within 24 hours you can cancel the e-Invoice (with a reason) — but the same invoice number cannot get a new IRN, so make a new invoice. After 24 hours, issue a credit note.
Does generating the IRN change my accounts?
No. The IRN, acknowledgement and QR code are saved on the invoice for printing and your records; the invoice amounts and your books stay as they are. The only charge is the wallet usage fee for the e-Invoice.
