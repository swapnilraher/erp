How to transfer stock from one branch to another

A transfer moves stock between your branches in four steps: request, review, dispatch and receive. Stock leaves the sending branch only when it is dispatched, and arrives at the other branch when it is received — with any shortage recorded.

⏱ About 4 minutes Menu: Items & stock → Stock transfers One item per transfer

Before you start

ℹ️

You need two branches (Settings → Branches & bill numbering), the item with its HSN code (see How to add an item) and enough stock of it at the sending branch.

Who does what: the owner, an admin, an accountant or a branch manager reviews (approves, asks a question or rejects). Staff tied to a branch can dispatch only from their own branch and receive only at their own branch.

Open Stock transfers

In the left menu click Items & stock → Stock transfers A, then + New transfer B.

The list shows each transfer with its date, number, the two branches and their GSTINs, the item, the status and the quantity. The tabs filter by step: Waiting for review, Questions, Ready to dispatch, In transit and Done.

Request the transfer

The window Transfer stock to another branch opens — Stock moves when it is dispatched.

  • From branch — the sending branch (fixed to your own branch if your user belongs to one). To branch A — the receiving branch.
  • Item B — search or scan. The stock at the sending branch is shown under the box. One item per transfer: make another transfer for each further item.
  • Quantity C — not more than the stock at the sending branch.
  • Value per unit (₹) D — filled with the item's purchase rate; it is the value used on the transfer papers and the e-Way Bill.
  • Purpose — Restock branch, Balance stock, Customer order or Return to main godown — and Notes for the other branch.

Click Send for review E. The transfer gets its number (for example ST-2026-00003) and waits for review. No stock has moved yet.

Review and approve

On the transfer's row click Review. The window shows the route, the quantity asked for and the requester's note; the item name on the transfer can still be changed. Then:

  • Ask a question A — for example to suggest a smaller quantity. The sending branch answers with Answer question (and may change the quantity), and the transfer comes back for review.
  • Reject B — with the reason in Notes.
  • Approve C — the transfer is ready to dispatch.

Dispatch

An approved transfer shows Dispatch A on its row. Click it when the goods are loaded.

In Dispatch from … enter the Transport A (road, rail, air or ship), the Vehicle number B and, if you like, the Transporter. Click Dispatch C: the stock leaves the sending branch now and the transfer is In transit. If the value and route need an e-Way Bill, the message after dispatch says so.

Receive at the other branch

When the goods arrive, click Receive on the transfer. For each item the window shows what was Sent:

  • Received A — count the goods and enter what arrived.
  • Difference B — Matches, Short by … or Extra ….
  • Remarks — for example what was short or damaged.

Click Receive C. The quantity received comes into the receiving branch's stock, and the transfer is Received, Received short or Received extra.

Details, e-Way Bill JSON and tax invoice

Click the transfer number or the eye icon to open its details: both branches with GSTIN and State, the items A with the quantity asked, sent and received and the value, the transport, any questions and who received it.

  • Download e-Way Bill JSON B — asks for the road distance (1 to 4000 km) and downloads the file to upload on the e-Way Bill portal.
  • Tax invoice C and Generate IRN — shown when the two branches have different GSTINs. Such a transfer is a supply between two registrations: the ERP books the GST at the sending branch on dispatch and the GST credit at the receiving branch on receipt. If e-invoicing applies to you, generate the IRN before printing — without it the invoice prints only as a draft.
💡

Branches under the same GSTIN (the usual case within one State): the transfer only moves stock between them; there is no tax invoice and no Tax invoice button.

Common questions

When does the stock actually move?
Only at dispatch (out of the sending branch) and at receipt (into the receiving branch). A request waiting for review, or an approved transfer not yet dispatched, does not change any stock.
The ERP says to add the HSN code first.
The HSN code goes on the transfer papers and the e-Way Bill, so it is required. Add it to the item under Items & stock → Items and make the transfer again.
I need to send several items.
Make one transfer for each item. The list, the search box and the tabs keep them together.
Fewer goods arrived than were sent.
Enter the quantity that actually arrived when receiving. Only that quantity comes into the receiving branch, the transfer is marked Received short, and your remarks are kept in its details.
