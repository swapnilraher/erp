How to run the pre-filing checks

One screen to look at before you file: does the tax in GSTR-1 agree with GSTR-3B, which supplier invoices in your imported GSTR-2B differ from your books or are missing, and how much ITC waits because a supplier has not reported a bill. You also record here the IMS action you took on the GST portal, so the match stays in step.

⏱ About 3 minutes Menu: GST → More GST → Pre-filing checks Import GSTR-2B for the month first

Before you start

ℹ️

Import the month's GSTR-2B from the file downloaded on the GST portal — see Match purchases with GSTR-2B. Without it the GSTR-2B part of the checks stays empty.

Nothing here files or changes anything. The checks read your books and the imported GSTR-2B. Recording an IMS action only keeps the match in step; it changes nothing in the books.

Open the checks

In the left menu open GST → More GST → Pre-filing checks A. It is the Checks before filing tab of the GST returns screen. Choose the Return period B (the example uses October 2026) and the GSTIN.

Read the four tiles

The tiles C give the state of the month at a glance:

TileWhat it tells you
GSTR-1 vs 3B (outward tax)Agree when the tax in GSTR-1 equals the outward tax in GSTR-3B; otherwise the difference in red.
2B invoices matchedHow many of the imported GSTR-2B invoices match your books.
ITC in books, not in 2B (deferred this month)The ITC of bills a supplier has not reported yet. GSTR-3B leaves it out until a GSTR-2B shows the bill.
IMS (on the portal): accepted / rejected / pending / no actionThe IMS actions you recorded for the month's GSTR-2B invoices.

If GSTR-1 was already recorded as filed and invoices changed in the books since, a yellow box lists what was added (report it in the next GSTR-1) and what was removed or changed (amend it by GSTR-1A or a credit note).

Go through the lists

  • Tax differs between GSTR-2B and the books A — the invoice is in both, but the tax is not the same. In the example the supplier reported ₹18,642.60 and your bill carries ₹18,675.00.
  • In GSTR-2B, not in the books B — a supplier reported an invoice you have not entered.
  • In the books this month, not in GSTR-2B (ITC at risk) C — your bill, not reported by the supplier yet.
💡

Correct your own mistakes in the bill itself (Purchases), then open the checks again — they are worked out fresh each time. For a supplier's mistake, ask the supplier to correct their return.

Record the IMS action

IMS is the Invoice Management System on the GST portal, where you accept, reject or keep pending each supplier invoice. Take the action on the portal, then record the same here with Accept, Reject or Keep pending D on the invoice's row.

Recording a rejection asks first A: do the same on the GST portal (IMS); this screen only keeps the reconciliation in step and changes nothing in the books.

⚠️

An invoice recorded as rejected is not counted as available ITC when bills are matched and in GSTR-3B. If such a bill is still taken in your books, the screen lists it in red — reverse the ITC or get a credit note.

The checks inside each return

Each return has its own checks too:

  • GSTR-1 — the Checks tile, the Checks of the return table (errors and warnings by section and document) and table 13 with missing invoice numbers. See Prepare GSTR-1.
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B — Books vs return compares the Output and Input GST ledgers with the return and lists any bill whose entry carries a different GST. See Prepare GSTR-3B.

Common questions

Does recording an IMS action here accept or reject the invoice on the GST portal?
No. The action is taken on the GST portal. Recording it here only keeps the GSTR-2B match and GSTR-3B in step with what you did there.
GSTR-1 vs 3B shows a difference. What do I check?
The comparison takes invoices, B2C sales, credit and debit notes and advances (table 11). A credit note to a registered buyer that the buyer has not yet accepted on IMS is reported in GSTR-1 but reduces GSTR-3B table 3.1(a) only once accepted. Open GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B for the month and compare their credit notes and advances.
The GSTR-2B part is empty.
No GSTR-2B has been imported for the month. Download it from the GST portal and import it on GST → GSTR-2B matching.
Do the checks change my books?
No. They only read your books and the imported GSTR-2B. Imported GSTR-2B rows never change the books.
