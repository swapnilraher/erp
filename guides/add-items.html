How to add an item with HSN, GST rate and prices

Every item is set up once with its HSN or SAC code, GST rate, unit and prices. Bills, orders and stock then take these from the item, so the GST on every invoice and purchase is worked out the same way.

⏱ About 2 minutes Menu: Items & stock → Items Shortcut: Ctrl+S saves

Before you start

ℹ️

Keep at hand the item's HSN code (goods) or SAC code (services) and its GST rate — the supplier's bill usually shows both — and your purchase rate, MRP and selling price.

Stock is not entered here. A new item starts at 0; stock comes in through a purchase bill or goods received, or as opening stock (see How to enter opening stock).

Open the item list

In the left menu click Items & stock → Items A, then + Add item B.

The list shows each item with its HSN and unit, barcode, category and brand, GST %, purchase rate, MRP, selling price, stock and status. The tab Low stock shows the items at or below their reorder level.

Enter the name, barcode and HSN code

The New item window has three parts: Item, Price & GST and Stock. Fields marked * are required.

  • Item name A — the name printed on bills.
  • Barcode B — scan or type the barcode on the pack. No barcode? Click Generate for an in-store code (it starts with 2, so it never clashes with a real product's code). More codes for the same item go in Other barcodes, separated by commas.
  • HSN / SAC code C — 4 to 8 digits.
  • Item code (SKU), Category and Brand / maker — optional; the category box offers the categories you already use.

Choose the GST rate and unit, and enter the prices

  • Prices include GST A — ticked when the selling price you enter already includes GST (as on a retail price tag). The GST invoice then works out the rate before GST. Untick it if you quote prices plus GST.
  • Type — Goods (HSN code) or Service (SAC code).
  • GST applies B — Taxable, Nil rated (0%), Exempt or Outside GST.
  • GST rate C — only the rates in force today are offered. Add Cess rate (%) if the item carries cess. Tick Bought under reverse charge only for items on which you, the buyer, pay the GST.
  • Unit D — from your unit list (each unit reports under its GST UQC).
  • Purchase rate (₹), MRP (₹) and Selling price (₹) E — all required. The selling price cannot be above the MRP. Wholesale from qty and Wholesale rate (₹) set a lower rate from a quantity.
💡

If the selling price is below the purchase rate, the ERP asks Selling price is below the purchase rate before saving — often a typing slip, but you can still choose Save anyway for a clearance price.

Set the reorder level and save

  • The note A reminds you that stock comes in through a purchase bill or goods received, with batch and expiry. Stock in hand changes only through bills, transfers and counts.
  • Reorder level B — when stock falls to this figure the item shows as Low.
  • Item group — optional, from your item groups.
  • Active (shown in billing) — untick to hide an item from billing. Track serial numbers (IMEI) — a serial number for every unit bought and sold.

Click Save item C or press Ctrl + S. If a required field is missing, it is marked in red and named in the message.

After saving

The message Item saved. Add stock through a purchase bill. appears and the item is in the list A with stock 0. On each row:

  • the printer icon prints barcode labels (MRP always, your selling price if you choose);
  • the pencil icon (or the item name) opens the item to edit;
  • ⋯ → Delete item. An item with stock or history cannot be deleted; the ERP offers to mark it inactive instead.

When a GST rate changes

Open the item and choose the new GST rate A. A box GST rate effective from appears: leave it blank for today, or enter the date the new rate applies from — bills dated earlier keep the old rate.

The GST rate history B lists the rate in force from each date; a bill is taxed at the rate on its date. With Earlier rate from, GST % and Record rate you can add the rate of an earlier period.

Common questions

Where do I find the HSN code and GST rate of an item?
Your supplier's purchase bill shows the HSN code and the GST rate of each item. Use the same on the item, so your sales and purchases match.
How do I add stock for a new item?
Enter the supplier's bill (see How to add a purchase bill), record goods received, or — when you start with the ERP — enter it as opening stock.
Can I add an item while making a bill?
Yes. The purchase bill and purchase order windows have + New item; in the purchase bill's item box you can also press Alt + C.
Why can't I save a selling price above the MRP?
A packaged item cannot be sold above its printed MRP, so the ERP stops it with Selling price is above the MRP.
