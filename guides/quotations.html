How to make a quotation

Give a customer a priced offer before the sale. The quotation shows the GST for the customer's state, has a valid-until date and your terms, and moves from draft to sent and accepted. When the customer agrees, one click turns it into a sales order. A quotation changes no stock and no accounts.

⏱ About 2 minutes Menu: Sales → Quotations Rates before GST

Before you start

ℹ️

Items must be in the item master (Items & stock → Items): the GST rate, HSN and unit come from there. Customers come from your customer list; a quotation can also go to someone who is not a customer yet.

Quotations, sales orders and deliveries are made by a supervisor or the accounts team. Counter staff (cashier and billing operator logins) cannot make them.

Open Sales → Quotations

In the left menu click Sales → Quotations A. The screen has three tabs C — Quotations, Sales orders and Delivery notes — for the whole path: quotation → sales order → delivery note → invoice.

Each quotation shows its number, date, customer, valid-until date, total and status. Click New quotation B.

Make the quotation

  • Customer A: search by name, mobile or GSTIN and press Enter. The line under it shows the customer's GSTIN and place of supply. Not a customer yet? Type the name in or a new customer's name.
  • Valid until B: the last day of the offer. Left empty, the quotation is valid for 30 days.
  • Items C: pick the item, type the Qty, check the Rate (before GST) and add a Disc % if you give one. The GST rate and the amount follow; a new empty line appears for the next item. If the customer has a price list or a scheme is running, its rate and discount are filled in.
  • Terms & conditions: delivery, payment and anything else you promise.

The totals D show the taxable value, the GST and the total. The GST is worked out for the place of supply shown under the customer — here a customer in Gujarat, billed from Pune.

Save and find it

Click Save quotation. The quotation gets its number (for example QT-2026-00015) and is listed as Draft A.

A quotation whose valid-until date has passed while it is still draft or sent shows Expired B. A customer typed by name only is marked (prospect). Use the search box to find a quotation by number or customer.

Sent, accepted or rejected

The ⋯ button of a row keeps the status up to date:

  • Mark sent — once you have given the quotation to the customer;
  • Accept or Reject — the customer's answer;
  • Edit — change a draft or sent quotation (the number stays);
  • Convert to order — see the next step. A converted quotation offers View order instead.

Convert to a sales order

When the customer agrees, choose Convert to order. The order takes the quotation's items, quantities and rates, and the tax is worked out again for the customer. Click Make the order A.

The quotation becomes Converted and the new sales order opens, ready to deliver or invoice — see Sales orders and deliveries.

💡

For a quotation made to a new customer (prospect), the ERP asks you to choose the customer from your customers before it makes the order. Add the customer first if needed — for example with + New customer on the GST invoice screen.

Common questions

Does a quotation reduce stock or go into the accounts?
No. A quotation is only an offer: no stock moves and nothing is posted. Stock leaves with the delivery note or the invoice, and the sale is booked with the invoice.
Are the quotation rates with or without GST?
Without. Type the rate before GST; the GST rate comes from the item, and the totals show the GST for the customer's state.
The quotation shows Expired. Can I still use it?
Yes. Expired only reminds you that the valid-until date has passed. A draft or sent quotation can still be edited (for example to give a new date), accepted or converted.
Can I change a quotation after it is accepted?
Only draft and sent quotations can be edited. An accepted quotation can still be rejected or converted; a converted or rejected one cannot be changed.
Can I skip the quotation and make an order directly?
Yes. New order is next to New quotation on the same screen. You can also bill straight away on Sales → GST invoice.
