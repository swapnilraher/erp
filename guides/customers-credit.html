How to manage customers and credit (udhar)

Decide who may buy on credit, how much they may owe and for how many days. The ERP checks the limit on every credit bill, shows who owes you in each branch, and keeps each customer's statement of bills and payments, ready to send on WhatsApp.

⏱ About 2 minutes per customer Menu: Sales → Customers Approve / block: owner, admin or branch manager

Before you start

ℹ️

This screen is for customers who buy on credit. Ordinary customers are added while you bill — with + New customer on the GST invoice or New customer on the counter (POS) — and can be given credit here at any time.

Approving credit, changing a limit and blocking are for the owner, an admin or a branch manager. A credit customer added by anyone else waits as Pending until one of them approves it.

Open Sales → Customers

In the left menu click Sales → Customers A. The screen Credit customers (udhar) opens, with Add credit customer B at the top right.

Dues by branch C adds up every unpaid credit bill in each branch — of credit customers and of other customers billed on credit (marked (customer)) — and names the parties who owe the most. Total due (all branches) is at the bottom.

Read the list

Each credit customer shows the member code, name, mobile, Credit limit, Due, Days of credit and Status. Search by name, mobile or member code; Export gives the list in Excel or CSV.

  • Over limit A: the dues are more than the credit limit.
  • Limit ₹0: no new credit: the limit is ₹0, so no new credit bills are allowed.
  • Status B: Active (credit allowed), Pending (waiting for a manager's approval) or Blocked.
  • The book icon C opens the customer's statement and payments; the pencil edits the customer.

Add a credit customer

Click Add credit customer. In Choose the customer type a name or mobile and click the customer A. Not in the list? Click Customer not in the list? Add a new customer and enter the name and the 10-digit mobile.

FieldWhat to enter
Member codeFilled in for you (MEM-…). Change it if you use your own codes; it can be searched on the counter.
Credit limit (₹) BThe most this customer may owe. ₹0 means no credit is allowed.
Due now (₹)Shown only; it comes from the bills and payments.
Days of credit CFor how many days the customer may keep the money, for example 30.
Credit status DWaiting for approval, Active (approved) or Blocked. Only a manager's choice is kept; anyone else's new customer is saved as waiting for approval.

GST details and save

For a registered business, check the GSTIN A. The ERP checks the last character as you type: ✓ GSTIN looks correct, or a red warning. Fill from GSTIN B fetches the legal name, trade name and state from the GSTIN (a live lookup, charged to your wallet).

Customer type, State (taken from the GSTIN), PAN, Name as on GST certificate and Trade name follow. For a customer without GSTIN this part is optional; choose the state. Click Save C.

⚠️

A customer can have only one credit account (the mobile number is checked). Once saved, a GSTIN cannot be removed from the credit customer.

Approve, block or remind

The ⋯ button of a row has the less frequent actions:

  • Approve credit — for a Pending customer; Unblock credit — for a Blocked one.
  • Send a payment reminder on WhatsApp — when the customer owes money and has a mobile number.
  • Billing / delivery addresses — the customer's address book.
  • Block credit — no new credit bills until a manager unblocks the account. Bills already made stay due.

Receive a payment

Click the book icon of the customer. The Statement window shows Credit limit, Due now and Credit still available A. Send on WhatsApp B sends a payment reminder for the dues; Open chat opens a ready message in WhatsApp instead, with a UPI link to pay when your UPI ID is saved.

Under Receive a payment C enter the Amount received (₹), choose Paid by (Cash, UPI, Cheque or Other), add the Ref. / cheque no. if you have one, and click Record payment.

The ERP asks once more and shows the dues after the payment. Click Record payment A to save it.

The payment is saved as a receipt, the customer's dues go down and the entry goes into your books (cash or bank against the customer's account).

The statement

Bills and payments lists every credit bill and payment with its date, reference and the running Balance due — the new payment is at the top A. Counter bills and tax invoices on credit both appear here.

WhatsApp B sends the statement as a PDF; Export statement C gives it in Excel, CSV or PDF, or prints it.

Common questions

Where do I add a customer who pays cash?
While billing: + New customer (or Alt + C) on the GST invoice, or New customer on the counter. Such customers then appear in Choose the customer here, if you later want to give them credit.
Does a ₹0 credit limit mean "no limit"?
No. ₹0 means no credit is allowed. Give a real amount to let the customer buy on credit.
What happens when a credit bill goes over the limit?
On the counter the credit check turns red and the Credit button is switched off. A cashier can send the bill to a manager for approval — see How to bill at the counter (POS).
The customer paid more than the dues. Is that allowed?
Yes. The ERP warns you, and the extra amount stays on the customer's account as an advance.
Who can approve credit or change the limit?
The owner, an admin or a branch manager. Other users can add credit customers, but their credit stays Pending until one of them approves it, and they cannot change a limit or the credit status.
