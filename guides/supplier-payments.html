How to pay a supplier and settle their bills

Record every payment you make to a supplier and say which bills it settles. The bills' balances, the supplier's balance and your cash or bank account are updated together, and anything paid beyond the bills is kept as an advance.

⏱ About 2 minutes Menu: Purchases → Payments made From a bill: the Pay bill (card) icon

Before you start

ℹ️

The supplier's bills should already be entered (see How to add a purchase bill), so the payment can be set against them.

Who can do it: supplier payments are recorded by the owner, an admin or an accountant. Keep the bank's UTR number or the cheque number at hand.

Open Payments made

In the left menu click Purchases → Payments made A, then + Pay supplier B. On any other Purchases screen, Pay supplier is also under the ⋯ button at the top.

The list shows every payment with its date, payment number, supplier, how it was paid, the reference, who entered it and the amount.

Choose the supplier, payment mode and reference

  • Supplier A — type the name or GSTIN and pick the supplier. The box on the right D shows how much you owe them (or the advance you have already paid).
  • Paying branch and Payment date — today by default.
  • Paid by B — Bank Transfer (NEFT / RTGS / IMPS), UPI / Online, Cheque or Cash.
  • Reference C — the UTR or cheque number, so the payment can be found and matched with the bank statement later.

Enter the amount and check the bills it covers

Type the Amount paid (₹) A, or click Pay all due B to pay every unpaid bill in full.

The table Bills this payment covers C lists the supplier's unpaid bills with the bill date, the supplier's bill number (and our number), the bill total and what is due. The amount is spread over the bills from the top of the list; change the Paying now (₹) figures to settle other bills — the amount paid then becomes their total.

Add Remarks if needed and click Save payment of ₹… D.

💡

Advance payment. If you pay more than the bills you tick, the window says the extra amount is kept as an advance with the supplier. If the supplier has no unpaid bills, the whole payment is kept as an advance. The supplier box then shows it as Advance with supplier.

After saving

The message Payment … saved. appears and the payment is at the top of the list A with its number, mode, reference and amount. At the same time:

  • each bill's balance goes down by its Paying now amount;
  • the amount you owe the supplier goes down;
  • the payment is posted to your books — from the cash account for a cash payment, otherwise from the bank / UPI account.
⚠️

Checks when you save: a bill can be paid only up to what is due on it; a payment cannot be dated in a locked period; if your company has set an approval limit for payments, a larger payment by someone other than the owner or an admin waits for approval and is not posted until it is approved; and if your company blocks negative cash, a cash payment cannot take the cash balance below zero.

Pay one bill from its row

In Purchases → Purchase bills each bill shows its payment status: a bill paid in full is Paid A, a bill paid in part is Part paid B with the rest in Balance due.

To pay a single bill, click the card icon on its row C (Pay this bill). The Pay supplier window opens with that supplier, and the bill's balance is filled in as the amount.

Common questions

I paid against several bills with one cheque.
Record one payment for the full cheque amount and set the Paying now figure of each bill it covers. The cheque number goes in Reference.
I paid the supplier in advance, before the bill.
Record the payment with no bill ticked: it is kept as an advance with the supplier, and the supplier box shows Advance with supplier. An advance against a purchase order can also be recorded from the order (⋯ → Advances); see purchase order and goods received.
Where do I see what I still owe a supplier?
In the supplier box of the Pay supplier window, in the Balance due column of Purchases → Purchase bills, and in the Supplier statements tab on the Purchases screen, which lists every bill and payment of one supplier.
Which bills should I pay first?
The Purchase checks tab on the Purchases screen shows the MSME payment rule (45 days) for your suppliers, next to goods received but not billed.
