How to make a sales invoice (GST tax invoice)

Make a GST tax invoice for a registered business (B2B) or a walk-in customer (B2C). The ERP picks CGST + SGST or IGST for you, keeps the invoice numbers in series and updates stock and the customer's balance when you save.

⏱ About 3 minutes Menu: Sales → GST invoice Shortcut: Ctrl+S saves

Before you start

ℹ️

Your business and branch are set up (Settings → Business details & GST). The invoice number comes from the branch's invoice prefix — for example SGT/26/00042.

Customers and items can be added in advance (Sales → Customers, Items & stock) or while you bill, from the invoice screen itself.

Open a new GST invoice

In the left menu click Sales → GST invoice A. You can also press Ctrl + K, type GST invoice and press Enter.

The next invoice number of your branch is shown at the top right. The final number is given by the server when you save, so the series has no gaps or duplicates.

Choose the customer

In Customer B type a few letters of the name, the mobile number or the GSTIN. Pick the customer with the arrow keys and Enter, or click it.

The customer's details appear on the right: GSTIN, phone, balance due, credit limit and payment terms. The badge next to the title changes to Registered buyer (B2B) when the customer has a GSTIN.

💡

New customer? Click + New customer, or press Alt + C in the search box — the name you typed is carried over. Enter the GSTIN and the party's name and address can be fetched for you.

Walk-in customer without GSTIN? Leave Customer empty and type the name or phone in Cash customer, or click Walk-in cash sale. The invoice is then a B2C invoice.

Check the date, payment mode and due date

  • Invoice date — today by default. A future date is not allowed.
  • Payment mode — Pay later (credit), Cash or UPI / bank. A registered business or a credit customer starts on credit.
  • Due date — worked out from the customer's terms (for example 30 days). Change it if you agreed otherwise.
  • Optional: Buyer PO no., PO date and Salesperson. Use Billing & delivery address when the goods go to a different ship-to address.

Add the items

In the Item box A type the product name, barcode or HSN and press Enter. The HSN code, GST rate, available stock and the selling rate come from the item master.

Enter the Qty B. The Rate (before GST) C is filled in — change it if needed, and add a Disc % if you give a line discount. A new empty line appears for the next item; you can also click + Add line.

💡

Selling at a price that already includes GST (for example the MRP)? Tick Rates include GST above the items — type the price with GST and the ERP works out the rate before GST.

Check the total and save

The Summary A shows the subtotal, any discount on the bill, the taxable value, the GST and the total in words. The tax type is chosen for you:

  • Same State (CGST + SGST) — the customer is in your branch's state.
  • Other State (IGST) — the customer is in another state (as in the example: Pune to Surat).

Need freight, packing or insurance on the bill? Use Other charges — they are taxed like the goods. The Transport section (transporter, vehicle, LR no., distance) is optional; what you enter there is used later for the e-Way Bill.

Click Save bill B or press Ctrl + S. If something is missing, a red Before saving, fix box lists it.

e-Invoice prompt for a registered buyer

For a customer with a GSTIN the ERP asks Generate the e-Invoice now?

  • If e-invoicing applies to your business, click Generate e-Invoice (IRN). The IRN and the signed QR code come from the e-invoice portal and are printed on the invoice. (Your e-Invoice API user must be saved once in GST → E-Invoice / E-Way Bill → GST Configuration.)
  • If it does not apply, click Skip e-Invoice A.

Print, download the PDF or send on WhatsApp

The print window opens next (you can open it any time with Print / PDF or Alt + P). Choose the format — A4 (tax invoice), A5 (half page) or Thermal 80 mm — and the copies (Original for Recipient, Duplicate for Transporter, Triplicate for Supplier).

From the same window you can make a payment link & UPI QR, send the PDF on WhatsApp, e-mail it, or use Preview, Download PDF and Print.

After saving

The invoice is saved and shown with its number, customer, total and due date. From here you can:

  • Print / PDF (Alt+P) B, Send on WhatsApp… or start a New invoice;
  • generate the e-Invoice (IRN) later, if you skipped it;
  • follow the yellow reminder A when the goods are worth more than the e-Way Bill limit — see How to generate an e-Way Bill;
  • open Go to bills list (Sales → Invoices) to reprint, return or cancel a bill, or Money to collect for the customer's dues.

When you save, the stock of the items goes down, the sale is posted to your books with its GST and — for a credit sale — the customer's balance goes up.

Common questions

Why does my invoice show IGST instead of CGST and SGST?
Because the customer is in another state. The ERP compares your branch's state with the place of supply (from the customer's GSTIN or address). Same state: CGST + SGST. Other state: IGST. The summary shows which one applies.
Can I bill a customer who has no GSTIN?
Yes. Leave the Customer box empty and type the name or phone in Cash customer, or click Walk-in cash sale. You can tick Save this customer to my customers to keep the name for next time. For fast counter sales use Sales → POS billing (F8).
Where do I find the invoices I have made?
In Sales → Invoices. From the list you can search, reprint, make a sales return or cancel a bill.
The customer is over the credit limit. What happens?
The summary shows how much the bill is over the limit. An owner or manager can still save it; counter staff are asked to get a manager to save the bill.
Can I add freight or packing charges?
Yes, under Other charges (freight, packing): Freight, Packing & forwarding, Insurance or Other charges. They carry GST like the goods on the bill.
