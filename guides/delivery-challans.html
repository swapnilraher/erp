How to make a delivery challan

When goods leave without a sale — on approval, for job work, for an exhibition or demo, or on consignment — make a delivery challan. It takes the goods out of stock and prints a challan to travel with them. It is not a bill and records no sale.

⏱ About 2 minutes Menu: Sales → Delivery challans Not a tax invoice

Before you start

ℹ️

Use a challan only when there is no sale yet. For goods that are sold, make a GST invoice, or a delivery note against a sales order — see Sales orders and deliveries.

The goods must be in the item master (Items & stock → Items). A challan cannot be dated in the future or in a locked accounting period.

Open Sales → Delivery challans

In the left menu click Sales → Delivery challans A. On this screen the menu is a row of icons; point at the Sales icon to see its screens. Then click New challan B.

Who, why and transport

  • Sent to (name) A: the customer or party who receives the goods (required). Mobile is optional, 10 digits.
  • GSTIN (if registered) B: when you type all 15 characters, Fill from GSTIN appears and fills in the party's name from the GSTIN (a live lookup, charged to your wallet).
  • Purpose C: Supply on Approval, Job Work, Exhibition / Demo, Consignment or Other. It is printed on the challan.
  • Vehicle no. D, Transporter and Delivery address — where and how the goods travel.

Add the items

Under Items: choose the item A by name or barcode (the list shows the stock in hand), type the Qty B and check the rate C — it starts at the item's selling rate and gives the estimated value printed on the challan. Click Add D; the line goes into the table below, where the bin icon removes it.

Add the next item the same way. Remarks takes delivery instructions — for example how long the goods are on approval. Then click Save challan E.

Save and print

The challan gets its number (for example DC-2026-00007) and the goods go out of stock. Click the challan number to open it: date, vehicle, transporter, address and the items with their estimated value. Click Print challan A.

The printout is headed DELIVERY CHALLAN — Issued under Rule 55 of CGST Rules, 2017 – Not a Tax Invoice. It shows the purpose, the challan number and date, vehicle and transporter, the consignee with mobile, GSTIN and address, each item with its HSN, quantity, estimated rate and value, the total goods value, your remarks, and spaces for the receiver's signature and stamp and the authorised signatory.

The challan list

The new challan is at the top A. Each challan shows its number, date, who it was sent to, purpose, vehicle, number of items, estimated value and status — Sent out, Billed, Returned or Cancelled. Search by challan number, name or mobile. The eye icon opens a challan and the printer icon B prints it again.

Common questions

Does a delivery challan create a sale or GST?
No. A challan only moves the stock. It is not a bill, records no sale and has no tax entry. The value on it is an estimate printed for the transport.
Does the stock go down when I save the challan?
Yes. The quantities on the challan leave the stock of the branch when it is saved.
Do challans appear in GST returns?
When you prepare GSTR-1 in the ERP, delivery challans are counted in the documents issued. Goods sent on approval that are still out after six months are listed there with a reminder to issue the tax invoice. You file the return on the GST portal.
What is the difference from a delivery note?
A delivery note belongs to a sales order: the goods are sold and the invoice is made from it. A delivery challan is for goods that are not sold (yet). See Sales orders and deliveries.
