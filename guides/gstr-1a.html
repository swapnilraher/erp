How to record GSTR-1A amendments

Made a mistake in an invoice that is already in your filed GSTR-1? Record the correction in Techstar ERP: pick the invoice, enter what was wrong, and download the GSTR-1A JSON. You upload that file and file GSTR-1A yourself on the GST portal. Your invoices and books are not changed.

⏱ About 3 minutes Menu: GST → More GST → GSTR-1A amendments Filed by you on the GST portal

Before you start

ℹ️

For invoices already reported in GSTR-1. As the screen says, GSTR-1A amends outward invoices and credit notes of a tax period before its GSTR-3B is filed.

The ERP records, you file. The correction is saved here and goes into a GSTR-1A JSON. Nothing is sent to the GST portal: you upload the JSON and file GSTR-1A there.

Your GSTIN is saved in Settings → Business details & GST — it decides CGST + SGST or IGST and is needed for the JSON.

Open GSTR-1A and load the month

In the left menu open GST → More GST → GSTR-1A amendments A. Choose the Return period B of the invoice — in the example September 2026, whose GSTR-1 is already filed — and click Load registers C.

Pick the invoice

In Invoice to correct A choose the invoice from the month's list. Its details as reported appear under Original invoice B: buyer GSTIN, place of supply, taxable value and GST rate.

The Type is set for you from the invoice — Invoice to a registered buyer (B2BA), Large invoice to an unregistered buyer in another State (B2CLA), Credit / debit note (CDNRA) or Export invoice (EXP-A). Change it only if it is wrong.

Enter the correction

Choose the Reason C — Wrong taxable value, Wrong buyer GSTIN, Wrong place of supply, Wrong GST rate or Other — and change the field that was wrong:

FieldWhat to enter
Corrected invoice numberFilled from the invoice; change only if the number was wrong.
Corrected buyer GSTINThe right GSTIN. Leave it blank for an unregistered buyer.
Corrected place of supplyThe right State. The ERP works out CGST + SGST (your own State) or IGST (another State) from it.
Corrected GST rateThe right rate.
Corrected taxable value (₹) DThe right taxable value — in the example ₹36,800 instead of ₹37,100.
Corrected cess (₹)If cess applies.

Save the correction

Click Save correction (box E above). The correction appears under Corrections recorded for Sep 2026 A: the type and the original invoice, the corrected details and reason, the new taxable value with the old one (Was), the corrected tax and the status Recorded.

Download the GSTR-1A JSON

Click Download GSTR-1A JSON B. The file (named like GSTR1A_<GSTIN>_2026-09.json) holds all the corrections of the month. Upload it yourself on the GST portal and file GSTR-1A there. Export Excel C gives the same corrections as a worksheet for your records or your CA.

⚠️

GSTR-1A changes what is reported, not your books. If the invoice itself must change in your accounts, use a credit or debit note as your accountant advises.

Common questions

Does saving a correction change my invoice or my books?
No. The correction is a separate record that goes into the GSTR-1A JSON. The invoice, the customer's balance and your ledgers stay as they are.
Does Techstar ERP file GSTR-1A?
No. It prepares the JSON. You upload it and file GSTR-1A on the GST portal yourself.
Why is my invoice not in the list?
The list shows the invoices of the month you loaded. Choose the invoice's month in Return period and click Load registers again.
How is the corrected tax worked out?
From the corrected taxable value and rate. If the corrected place of supply is your own State, the tax is split into CGST and SGST; otherwise it is IGST.
