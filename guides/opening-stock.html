How to enter opening stock (item by item or from a CSV file)

When you start with the ERP, enter the stock you already hold: quantity and cost rate of each item, by branch and batch. Its value becomes the opening balance of Stock-in-Hand in your accounts, so the stock and the books agree from day one.

⏱ About 3 minutes Menu: Items & stock → Opening stock Owner, admin or accountant

Before you start

ℹ️

The items must already exist (see How to add an item), and you need a stock count on the day your books start, with the cost rate of each item.

Who can do it: the owner, an admin or an accountant. The books start date is set under Banking & accounts → Financial year & lock.

Until a month is locked. Opening stock can be changed until a month of the books is locked. After that it is frozen; bring stock in with a purchase or a physical stock count instead.

Open Opening stock

In the left menu click Items & stock → Opening stock A.

The strip at the top B shows the date your books start on, the value of the opening stock entered item by item, and the opening stock in accounts — the opening balance of Stock-in-Hand. They should match; if not, the strip says Differs from the items. Below it, the table lists the opening lines with branch, batch, expiry, quantity, rate and value.

Add an opening line

In Add or change an opening line:

  • Item A — search the item or scan its barcode (services are not offered: they have no stock).
  • Branch B — the branch that holds this stock.
  • Qty C — the quantity counted.
  • Rate (cost) D — the cost per unit; the item's purchase rate is filled in, change it to your actual cost.
  • Batch and Expiry — only for items kept by batch (medicines, food, paints).

Click Save line E. The message Opening stock saved. appears.

Check the line and the totals

The new line is in the table A with its value (quantity × rate), and the totals at the top B go up by the same amount — in the item list and in the accounts.

  • To change a line, enter the same item, branch and batch again with the right quantity or rate: it replaces the old line.
  • To remove a line, click its bin icon and confirm.
  • Use the branch box above the table to see one branch at a time.

Import many lines from a CSV file

For many items, use Import (CSV) lower on the screen. Click Download a sample file A to see the format, then paste your rows in the box B, one row per line:

barcode, qty, rate, batch, expiry (YYYY-MM-DD), branch code

  • Batch, expiry and branch may be left blank; a blank branch takes the branch chosen above the table.
  • Put text that contains a comma in double quotes. A header row is skipped.
  • Add / update these lines C adds the rows and replaces any existing line for the same item, branch and batch.
  • Replace all openings D replaces the whole opening stock with the rows in the box (it asks you to confirm first).

Fix the rows the ERP reports

Every row is checked before anything is saved. If a row is wrong, nothing is saved and the box A lists each problem with its row number — for example a quantity that is not a number, an expiry not written as YYYY-MM-DD, or a branch code that does not exist. Correct the rows and click the button again.

Common questions

Why does the ERP ask for a cost rate?
The quantity × cost rate of every line is the value of your opening stock. That value is the opening balance of Stock-in-Hand in your accounts, so your profit is right from the first month.
The screen says some items got stock on the item master, not as opening stock.
Older data may carry stock on the item itself. Its value is not in the opening stock in the accounts, so the first period would show it as profit. Choose the branch that holds that stock above the table and click Make them opening stock.
I found more stock after a month was locked.
Opening stock is frozen once a month of the books is locked. Bring the stock in with a purchase, or with a physical stock count under Items & stock.
Can I enter the same item for two branches or two batches?
Yes — one line per item, branch and batch. The same item, branch and batch can appear only once; entering it again replaces the line.
