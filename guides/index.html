Techstar ERP help guides

Step-by-step guides with screenshots for every part of Techstar ERP: billing, purchases, stock, accounts, GST and reports.

24 guides Sales Purchases Items & stock GST

Sales 8

Make a sales invoiceCustomer, items, CGST / SGST or IGST, save, print and send on WhatsApp. Bill at the counter (POS)Scan or search items, discount, cash and change, UPI, card or credit, hold and recall bills. Customers and credit (udhar)Credit customers with limit and days, approve or block credit, statement, reminders and payments received. Find, reprint or cancel an invoiceAll bills in one list: search, view, print or PDF, WhatsApp, return items or cancel a bill. Make a quotationQuote rates before GST with validity and terms, mark it sent or accepted, then convert it to a sales order. Sales orders and deliveriesBook the order with PO and delivery date, deliver in parts with delivery notes, invoice each delivery. Delivery challansSend goods out without a bill — on approval, job work, demo or consignment — and print the Rule 55 challan. Sales return and credit noteTake goods back against the bill, or give value-only credit for a rate difference, discount or damage.

Purchases 5

Add a purchase billSupplier's bill number and date, items, GST credit, freight and payment. Add suppliersGSTIN look-up, GST status, State, payment terms, opening balance and bank account. Purchase order and goods received (GRN)Order from a supplier, approve it, then record what arrived: accepted, free and rejected quantities. Pay a supplierBank transfer, UPI, cheque or cash; settle one or more bills, or keep an advance. Purchase return and debit noteSend goods back against a bill, or debit a rate difference or discount — with or without GST.

Items & stock 3

Add items with HSN, GST and pricesName, barcode, HSN / SAC, GST rate, unit, purchase rate, MRP, selling and wholesale price. Enter opening stockQuantity and cost rate on the day the books start, by branch and batch, or from a CSV file. Transfer stock between branchesRequest, review, dispatch with vehicle details and receive — with shortages, e-Way Bill JSON and tax invoice.

GST 8

Generate an e-Invoice (IRN)One-time e-Invoice API user, generate the IRN and QR code, print, or cancel within 24 hours. Generate an e-Way BillOne-time portal login, Part B transport details, print, update vehicle, extend or cancel. Prepare GSTR-1GSTR-1 prepared from your invoices: check it, download the JSON, file on the GST portal, record the ARN. Prepare GSTR-3BGSTR-3B from your books with ITC limited to GSTR-2B, the set-off, copy tables for the portal, record the ARN. Match purchases with GSTR-2BImport the GSTR-2B file from the GST portal and see which bills match, differ or are missing. Books are not changed. GSTR-1A amendmentsCorrect an invoice already reported in GSTR-1 and download the GSTR-1A JSON to upload on the GST portal. GST payments and cash ledgerRecord the PMT-06 challan you paid on the GST portal and use the electronic cash ledger for the GSTR-3B tax payment entry. Pre-filing checksBefore you file: GSTR-1 against GSTR-3B, GSTR-2B against your purchase bills, and the IMS action on each supplier invoice.

Screenshots are from Techstar ERP with a demo company (Shree Ganesh Traders, Pune). All names, GSTINs, amounts and document numbers are sample data.
