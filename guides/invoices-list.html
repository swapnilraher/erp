How to find, reprint or cancel an invoice

Every sales bill — GST invoices and counter (POS) bills — is in one list. Search it by bill number, customer or mobile, open a bill to see it, print it again in A4, A5 or thermal size, send the PDF on WhatsApp, or cancel it. When a bill is cancelled, its stock comes back and the amount is reversed in your books.

Before you start

ℹ️

Bills are made on Sales → GST invoice and Sales → POS billing. This list shows them all; nothing is changed until you return items or cancel a bill.

Cancelling a bill is for the owner, an admin, an accountant or a branch manager. Other users can ask for it; the request goes to their supervisor for approval.

Open Sales → Invoices

In the left menu click Sales → Invoices A. On this screen the menu is a row of icons; point at the Sales icon to see its screens. The screen is called Bills & sales returns.

Find the bill

The totals at the top A are for the dates shown: Total billed (cancelled bills not counted), Net sales (billed less returns), Returns and Cancelled bills.

  • Search by bill no., customer name or mobile… B — type part of the number or name.
  • The status list C: All bills, Active, With returns or Cancelled.
  • The dates D: the last 30 days are shown at first. Change the dates to find an older bill.

Each row shows the bill number, date, customer, branch, payment, quantity, total and status — Active, Part returned, Fully returned or Cancelled, with e-Invoice and e-Way badges where they exist. The buttons at the end E are: view, print / share, send on WhatsApp and ⋯ for more.

💡

Several bills in one print: tick the bills and click Print selected. Each invoice is printed on its own page in one document (up to 100 bills at a time).

Open the bill

Click the bill number (or the eye icon). The bill opens in a panel on the right with its status, the customer (with GSTIN and phone), date, branch, payment, items, taxable value, GST and the total in words.

At the top: Print / PDF A and Send on WhatsApp B. At the foot C: Return items and Cancel bill.

Reprint, PDF, WhatsApp or e-mail

Print / PDF opens the print window.

  • Format A: A4 (tax invoice), A5 (half page) or Thermal 80 mm.
  • Copies B: Original for Recipient, Duplicate for Transporter, Triplicate for Supplier.
  • Make link & QR makes a payment link and UPI QR for the customer.
  • Send on WhatsApp sends the bill PDF to the number shown; E-mail invoice mails the A4 original as a PDF.
  • Preview, Download PDF or Print C. Print, PDF and WhatsApp use the same invoice layout.

Return items or cancel

The ⋯ button of a row has the same two actions as the foot of the bill panel:

  • Return items — the customer brings back some or all of the goods. Only the returned items go back to stock and are credited; the bill itself stays. See Sales return and credit note.
  • Cancel bill — the whole bill is wrong (for example a duplicate) and must be undone.

Cancel a bill

The Cancel this bill window says what happens: the whole bill is cancelled, all its items go back to stock, the full amount is reversed in the accounts, and the bill shows as Cancelled.

If the bill has an e-Invoice or an e-Way Bill, a yellow note A reminds you to cancel those on their portal as well. The e-Invoice portal allows cancelling an IRN only within 24 hours of generating it; after that, issue a credit note instead of cancelling the bill.

Choose the Reason B — Billing mistake / duplicate bill, Order cancelled before goods were handed over, Payment failed / cheque bounced or Wrong customer billed — and click Yes, cancel bill C. Keep bill leaves it as it is.

⚠️

A cancelled bill is not deleted: it keeps its number and stays in the list as Cancelled. It is not counted in Total billed.

Common questions

I can't find an old bill. Why?
Only the dates in the date boxes are searched, and the list starts with the last 30 days. Set an earlier From date and search again.
Should I cancel the bill or return items?
Cancel when the whole bill should not exist (a mistake, a duplicate, the wrong customer). Return items when goods really came back: the bill stays and only the returned goods are credited.
Why was my cancellation sent for approval?
Only the owner, an admin, an accountant or a branch manager can cancel a bill. For other users the ERP sends the cancellation, with the reason, to their supervisor for approval.
The bill has an e-Invoice older than 24 hours. What now?
The IRN can no longer be cancelled on the e-Invoice portal, so the ERP's note tells you to issue a credit note instead of cancelling the bill — see Sales return and credit note.
Can I send the bill on WhatsApp if the customer has no mobile on it?
Yes. The WhatsApp button then opens the print window; type the number in WhatsApp number and click Send on WhatsApp.
