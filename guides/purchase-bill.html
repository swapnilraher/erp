How to add a purchase bill (supplier invoice)

Enter the supplier's bill exactly as it is printed. When you save, the stock comes in, the GST credit (ITC) is recorded and the amount you owe the supplier is updated.

⏱ About 3 minutes Menu: Purchases → Purchase bills Shortcut: F9 opens a new bill

Before you start

ℹ️

Keep the supplier's bill in front of you. You will need the supplier, the bill number, the bill date, the items with quantity and rate, and the GST on the bill.

Suppliers and items can be added in advance (Purchases → Suppliers, Items & stock) or while entering the bill.

Open a new purchase bill

In the left menu click Purchases → Purchase bills A, then + New purchase bill B. From any screen you can also press F9 or click + Purchase in the top bar.

The list shows every bill with its supplier, our bill number, the supplier's bill number, GST, total and balance due, and how much you owe suppliers in all.

Choose the supplier and enter the bill details

  • Supplier A — type the name or GSTIN and pick the supplier. Their GSTIN, state, usual terms and balance payable appear on the right. Not in the list yet? Use Add supplier.
  • Receiving branch — the branch where the goods arrive.
  • Against GRN — choose the goods received note if the goods were received first; otherwise leave Not against a GRN.
  • Supplier's bill no. B — exactly as printed. If the same bill of this supplier is already entered, the ERP warns you before you save it twice.
  • Supplier's bill date C — the date printed on the bill.
  • Book on — leave blank to use the bill date. This is the date in your books and the month in which the GST credit is taken.
  • Payment terms — for example Due in 30 days, or Paid at purchase (cash, UPI, bank) when you pay on the spot.

Add the items

For each line of the supplier's bill:

  1. In Item A search the item or scan its barcode. The HSN, GST %, MRP and selling price come from the item master. A new item? Press Alt + C or click + New item.
  2. Enter the Qty B and the Rate (₹) C before GST, as on the bill. Add Free qty for scheme bonus units and Disc % for a trade discount.
  3. Fill Batch no. and Expiry if they are printed (medicines, food).
  4. Click + Add D, or press Enter on the last box.

Check the lines

Each added line shows the quantity, free quantity, rate, discount, landed cost per unit, MRP and selling price, and the line total. You can still change the quantity or rate in the table.

The Use column says how the item is used:

  • For resale — normal stock with GST credit.
  • No GST credit — items whose GST cannot be claimed by law, for example food or a car.
  • Fixed asset — bought for use in the business, not for sale (with or without GST credit).

Add charges, tax details and payment (if any)

  • Freight & other charges (no GST) — spread over the items as part of their landed cost.
  • Charges billed with GST — freight or other charges on which the supplier charged GST.
  • Reverse charge — tick it when you pay the GST yourself (for example goods transport by a GTA).
  • Paid now and Paid from — if you paid the supplier at the time of purchase.
  • Place of supply — normally your branch's state. GST charged by another state on a supply received there cannot be claimed as credit.

Check the total and save

The Summary A shows the taxable value, the GST on the bill, the GST credit you can claim and the bill total. Compare the bill total with the supplier's bill. The tax type is shown at the top: Same State (CGST + SGST) or Other State (IGST).

Click Save purchase bill B or press Ctrl + Enter.

After saving

The bill appears at the top of the list with our own number (for example PE-26-27-0019), the supplier's bill number, the GST and the balance due. At the same time:

  • the stock of each item goes up at the receiving branch;
  • the GST credit (ITC) and the amount you owe the supplier are updated in your books.

When you pay the supplier, record it with the Pay bill (card) icon on the bill's row, or under Purchases → Payments made.

Common questions

The supplier is in another state. What changes?
Nothing for you to do: the bill is taxed as IGST and the summary shows Other State (IGST). The GST credit is recorded as IGST.
The supplier is unregistered or a composition dealer.
Such a supplier cannot charge GST, so no GST credit is taken on the bill. If reverse charge applies, tick Reverse charge; for an unregistered supplier a self-invoice number is issued.
What is the “Book on” date?
The date on which the bill goes into your books and stock, and the month in which its GST credit is claimed. Leave it blank to use the supplier's bill date while that month is still open.
I returned some goods to the supplier.
Make a debit note under Purchases → Debit notes against the bill.
How do I check my purchase bills against GSTR-2B?
Download the GSTR-2B file from the GST portal and import it in GST → GSTR-2B matching. The ERP compares it with your purchase bills and shows what matches and what does not. The GSTR-2B file is only used for checking — it never creates or changes purchase bills; you enter them yourself as shown above.
