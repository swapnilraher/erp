How to prepare GSTR-3B in Techstar ERP

Techstar ERP works out GSTR-3B from your sales and purchase bills: the tax on sales, the input tax credit (only for the bills your imported GSTR-2B shows), interest and late fee, and how much to pay in cash after setting off the credit. You copy the tables to the GST portal, file there and record the ARN.

⏱ About 5–10 minutes Menu: GST → GSTR-3B Import GSTR-2B for the month first

Before you start

ℹ️

The ERP prepares, you file. GSTR-3B is worked out from your books. This software does not connect to the GSTN and does not file returns: you file GSTR-3B on the GST portal and record its ARN here.

Import the month's GSTR-2B first on GST → GSTR-2B matching (see Match purchases with GSTR-2B). The ITC in table 4 is limited to the bills it shows. GSTR-2B is used read-only: it never creates or changes purchases, journals or ledgers.

All sales and purchase bills of the month are entered — see sales invoice and purchase bill.

Open GSTR-3B

In the left menu click GST → GSTR-3B A. Choose the Return period B (the example uses October 2026), the Financial year and the GSTIN.

In the grey bar C, Date you will pay the tax (for interest / late fee) is today by default. Next to it you see the due date and, if you pay late, the days late; the interest and late fee in table 5.1 are worked out from this date.

Check the ITC against GSTR-2B

The box A shows how many GSTR-2B documents are imported for the month and the ITC of your books against what goes into table 4(C). In the example: ITC in books ₹39,375.00, table 4(C) ₹29,835.00.

ITC in books, not in 2B (deferred) is the credit of bills your supplier has not yet reported. Click Show list to see them B with the reason. Deferred ITC is taken in the month whose GSTR-2B shows the bill — the ERP brings it forward then.

The tiles C sum up the return: Output tax, Reverse charge tax, Net ITC (limited to 2B) and Cash to pay (with interest / fee). The two Books vs return boxes compare the Output and Input GST ledgers of the month with the return.

⚠️

No GSTR-2B imported for the month? The box turns red and the ITC shown is the books' figure. Copy for portal and the tax payment entry then ask for a reason (at least 10 characters), which is kept in the audit log. Only the owner, an admin or the accountant can go ahead without GSTR-2B.

Tables 3.1 and 3.2

3.1 Outward and reverse-charge inward supplies A gives the taxable value and IGST / CGST / SGST / cess of your sales, zero-rated and exempt supplies and inward supplies under reverse charge. 3.2 C lists inter-State sales to unregistered persons, composition dealers and UIN holders by place of supply.

Click Copy for portal B next to a table, then paste it into the same table of GSTR-3B on the GST portal and check the figures there.

Table 4 — eligible ITC

Table 4 shows the ITC by kind: imports, reverse charge and (A)(5) All other ITC A, less reversals in 4(B), giving (C) Net ITC Available B. The yellow line C shows the deferred ITC that is not in 4(A) this month.

Below it, table 5 shows exempt, nil-rated and non-GST inward supplies.

💡

ITC reversals (for example a supplier not paid within 180 days) are posted from the ITC register tab of the same screen and appear in table 4(B).

Set-off and cash to pay

5.1 Interest and late fee · 6.1 Payment of tax A shows, for each head, the tax payable, how much is paid through ITC (IGST, CGST, SGST, cess credit) and how much is left to pay in cash, with any interest and late fee. The credit carried forward is shown below the table.

The set-off is worked out for the least cash: IGST credit first on IGST, then on CGST / SGST; CGST credit on CGST then IGST; SGST credit on SGST then IGST — never CGST against SGST; reverse-charge tax is paid in cash.

Pay the cash by a PMT-06 challan on the GST portal and record it in the ERP — see GST payments and cash ledger. Then Record the tax payment entry B posts the set-off in your books: the output GST ledgers are cleared against the input GST and the electronic cash ledger. The ERP refuses it if the cash ledger does not hold enough cash.

File on the portal and record the ARN

File GSTR-3B on the GST portal with your own login. Then, under Filed on the GST portal? Record the ARN, type the 15-character ARN and the filing date and click Mark filed D.

Keep Also record the tax payment entry (record the PMT-06 challan first) C ticked to post the set-off at the same time (skip it if you already used Record the tax payment entry). Marking it filed keeps the return with its ARN and locks the month for sales documents.

Common questions

Why is the ITC in table 4 less than the GST on my purchase bills?
Because the ITC is limited to the bills your imported GSTR-2B shows. A bill the supplier has not reported yet is listed as deferred and is taken in the month whose GSTR-2B shows it.
Does Techstar ERP file GSTR-3B or pay the tax?
No. It prepares the figures and lets you copy each table. You pay the challan and file the return on the GST portal, then record the challan and the ARN in the ERP.
How are interest and late fee worked out?
From the date you enter in Date you will pay the tax: interest on the tax paid in cash after the due date, and the late fee per day after the due date up to the cap shown next to it.
I file quarterly (QRMP). Does the ERP know?
Set GST return frequency on the same screen to Quarterly (QRMP) if your business is eligible. The due dates, interest and late fee then follow the quarterly dates.
Does importing GSTR-2B change my books?
No. GSTR-2B is used only to limit the ITC shown in GSTR-3B and to match bills. Your purchases, journals and GST ledgers change only when you post entries yourself (for example the tax payment entry).
