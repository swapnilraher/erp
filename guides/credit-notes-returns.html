How to make a sales return and a credit note

Two ways to give a customer money back. When goods come back, make a sales return on the bill: the goods go back into stock and the bill is credited with its GST. When nothing comes back — a rate difference, a discount after the sale, damage in transit — issue a credit note for the value only.

⏱ About 2 minutes Menu: Sales → Invoices / Sales → Credit notes Shortcut: Ctrl+F8 opens Credit notes

Before you start

ℹ️

Goods came back? Use a sales return (step 1). Only money — rate difference, discount, damage, goodwill? Use a credit note (step 4). The credit note window itself says: Returned goods: use Sales Returns.

To undo a whole bill that should not exist, cancel it instead — see How to find, reprint or cancel an invoice.

Goods came back: Return items

Open Sales → Invoices and find the bill. Click its ⋯ button and choose Return items A. (The same button is at the foot of the bill when you open it.)

Quantity, reason and refund

The Return items window lists each line of the bill with Sold, Returned (earlier returns), Can return, rate and GST %. Only the returned items go back to stock and are credited; the original bill stays.

  • Return qty A: how many pieces come back (at most what can still be returned). The credit for the line appears next to it.
  • Reason B: Damaged or defective, Extra quantity sent, Wrong item or size, Customer changed their mind or Failed quality check.
  • Value goes back as C: Adjust in the customer's account (for a credit bill it reduces the bill), Refund in cash or Refund by bank / UPI. A walk-in bill without a customer from the customer master can only be refunded, and a credit bill is refunded only up to what was paid on it.

The box on the right shows the taxable value, the GST reversed and the amount to refund or credit. Click Save return D — the button shows the amount.

The Returns tab

The return gets its number (for example SR-2026-00004) and is listed on the Returns tab A B with the bill number, items returned, taxable value, GST reversed and total credited. The bill now shows Part returned or Fully returned, and Returns and Net sales at the top change with it.

When the return is saved, the goods go back into stock, the sale and its GST are reversed in your books and — when adjusted in the account — the customer's dues go down. Click the return number to see it: it is shown as Return (credit note) with its journal number.

No goods back: Sales → Credit notes

In the left menu click Sales → Credit notes A, or press Ctrl + F8. Each note shows its number, date, customer, bill number, reason, taxable value, GST, total credit and status — Issued, Adjusted or Cancelled, with how the credit was given. Click New credit note B.

Make the credit note

  • Invoice credited A: search by invoice number or customer. The line under it shows the invoice date, customer, CGST + SGST or IGST, what is left to credit and until when a GST note is possible. A note can also cover several invoices of the same customer: add them and give each its share (or use Split the credit oldest first).
  • Type B: GST credit note (reduces output tax) — needs an invoice — or Commercial credit note (no GST). Without an invoice, or after the last date shown, only a commercial note can be made; then choose the customer or type a walk-in name.
  • Reason: Post-Sale Discount, Rate Difference, Damaged / Defective Goods, Goodwill Adjustment or Sales Return. For a GST note with a post-sale discount, the ERP asks for the agreement or scheme the discount was agreed under before the sale, and its date.
  • Credit given as C: Adjust in the customer's account, Refund in cash or Refund by bank / UPI.
  • The lines D: description, GST rate (the rates of the invoice), taxable value; tax and total are worked out. Another rate adds a line for a second GST rate.

If something is missing, a red list says what. Click Issue credit note E. The note gets its number (for example CN-2026-00006).

Print, cancel and the buyer's IMS action

Click the note number to open it. Print note B prints the GST CREDIT NOTE (issued under Section 34 of the CGST Act) with the original invoice number and the buyer's GSTIN.

For a buyer with a GSTIN, the box A Buyer's action on IMS follows the note: from 1-Oct-2025 a credit note reduces your GST only after the buyer accepts it on IMS (the GST portal's Invoice Management System). Look the action up on the GST portal and record it here — Pending, Accepted, Deemed accepted or Rejected, with the date — and click Save. The badge then shows Reduces output tax or Does not reduce output tax yet. The ERP does not connect to the portal; you record what the portal shows.

⚠️

Cancel note C (owner, admin, accountant or branch manager) asks for a reason. The note's journal is reversed on its own date, the invoice's balance and the customer's account are restored, and the note leaves the GST register. It is not possible once that month's GST return is filed.

Common questions

Sales return or credit note — which one?
A sales return when goods come back: it puts them back into stock. A credit note when only the value changes and no goods come back (rate difference, discount, damage, goodwill).
Can I give credit without an invoice?
Only as a commercial credit note (no GST): choose the customer, or type a walk-in customer's name and refund in cash or by bank. A GST credit note must correct an invoice.
Why is the GST credit note option greyed out?
Either no invoice is chosen, or the last date for a GST note on that invoice has passed (it is shown under the invoice). Then only a commercial credit note can be made.
The bill has an e-Invoice older than 24 hours. Can I still cancel it?
The IRN can no longer be cancelled on the e-Invoice portal, so the ERP tells you to issue a credit note instead of cancelling the bill — use the steps above.
Can one credit note cover several invoices?
Yes, for the same customer, branch and kind of supply. Add the other invoices under the first one and give each invoice its share; the shares must add up to the credit.
