FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us or WhatsApp +91 95790 05645.

1. Is Techstar ERP 100% web-based? +
Yes. It runs in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone — nothing to install. You can also install it as an app from Chrome or Edge.
2. Can I manage multiple branches? +
Yes. The Business plan includes 2 branches and the Enterprise plan up to 5, each with its own GSTIN and invoice series if needed, branch transfers and branch-wise reports.
3. Does Techstar check GSTINs? +
Yes — GSTINs are checked for format, check digit and state code when you import parties or add customer addresses, and again in the GSTR-1 checks. A live lookup also fills the party's legal name, address and registration status from the GST system.
4. Can I create e-Invoices from Techstar? +
Yes — on every plan Techstar generates the e-Invoice directly from your B2B invoice through a licensed GST service provider. The IRN and signed QR print on your invoice; cancellation within 24 hours is done from the ERP too. No portal login or file upload.
5. Can I create e-Way Bills? +
Yes — straight from the ERP, from an invoice, delivery challan or sales / purchase return, with transporter and vehicle details. Part-B updates, validity extension and cancellation are done in Techstar as well. An e-Way Bill is generally needed when goods worth more than ₹50,000 move.
6. How do users and roles work? +
There are 9 ready roles — Owner, Admin, Accountant, Branch Manager, Billing Operator, Cashier, Purchase Manager, Store Keeper and Auditor (read-only). Users can be limited to their branch.
7. Can I customise my invoice? +
Yes. Add your logo, bank details, terms, declaration, signatory and footer, and print in A4, A5 or 80 mm thermal with Original / Duplicate / Triplicate copies.
8. Which plans include e-Invoice and e-Way Bill? +
Every plan — Starter, Business and Enterprise. Each portal action is charged from the prepaid wallet at the rates shown in the app. All plan prices include GST.
9. Who must generate e-Invoices? +
Businesses whose aggregate annual turnover in any year since 2017-18 exceeds ₹5 crore must e-invoice B2B supplies. Businesses with turnover of ₹10 crore or more must report each invoice on the IRP within 30 days. Check with your CA for your case.
10. Which printers can I use? +
Any printer installed on your computer: 80 mm thermal for receipts, A4 or A5 for tax invoices. You can bulk-print up to 100 invoices at a time or download a PDF.
11. Does it prepare GST returns? +
Yes. GSTR-1 / 1A JSON with checks, the GSTR-3B working with set-off, GSTR-2B reconciliation with IMS status, the ITC register and the GSTR-9 / 9C working. You file on the GST portal and record the ARN in Techstar.
12. How do low-stock and expiry checks work? +
The reorder report lists items at or below their reorder level with a suggested quantity, and the expiry report shows batches that are expired or about to expire. Expired batches are never sold.
13. Can I bring my data from Tally or Excel? +
Yes. Import customers, suppliers, products, opening stock, ledger openings and bill-wise outstanding from Excel / CSV templates — including data exported from Tally. Every row is checked before anything is saved.
14. Is my data safe? +
Each company's data is isolated by database-level security, every change is in the audit trail, data is stored in India (Mumbai), and owners can download a full backup of their company data any time.
15. Do you help with setup? +
Yes — we help you set up your company, masters and opening balances on a screen-sharing call.
16. Can I send invoices on WhatsApp? +
Yes — the invoice PDF goes straight to your customer's WhatsApp from Techstar, and you can send payment reminders for outstanding bills the same way. No need to download and forward files from your phone.
17. Do I need e-Invoicing? +
e-Invoicing is mandatory for B2B invoices of businesses whose aggregate turnover has crossed ₹5 crore in any financial year since 2017-18. Techstar generates the IRN for such invoices directly from the ERP; your CA can confirm whether it applies to you.
