Help Center FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We have answers. If you don't find what you need here, contact support.

1. Is Techstar ERP 100% web-based? +
Yes, Techstar ERP is entirely cloud-based. You can access the system securely through any desktop, tablet, or mobile browser. No local downloads or installation needed.
2. Can I manage multiple outlets/branches? +
Absolutely. Multi-branch management is a core Techstar USP. You can connect, manage, and consolidate inventory, billing, sales, and accounting data from a single account.
3. Does Techstar support GSTIN verification? +
Yes. Techstar ERP supports GSTIN verification within the billing workflow. You can verify customer and supplier GSTIN details including registration status, business name and state information. Note: GSTIN data reflects publicly available information and may not always be real-time.
4. Can I generate e-Invoices from Techstar ERP? +
Yes. Techstar ERP supports e-Invoice generation for eligible sales transactions. You can manage IRN details and QR code information connected to your invoices. Eligibility for e-Invoicing depends on your business turnover and applicable government rules. Please contact us for details about your specific plan.
5. Can I create and manage e-Way Bills? +
Yes. Techstar ERP supports e-Way Bill creation and management for goods in transit. You can enter transporter details, vehicle information and track e-Way Bill validity. Requirements for e-Way Bills depend on applicable government rules and goods value thresholds.
6. How does Role-Based Access Control work? +
System owners can configure exact permissions. For example, a POS Cashier can only see checkout screens, Accountants get access to ledger reports, and Viewers can only view dashboards.
7. Can I customize invoice formats? +
Yes. Techstar offers customizable invoice layouts. Add your brand logo, business address, personalized headers, authorized signature, terms & conditions, and local tax fields (CGST/SGST/IGST).
8. Are e-Invoice and e-Way Bill features in all plans? +
e-Invoice and e-Way Bill support is available in the Techstar Growth and Enterprise plans. Eligibility also depends on your business profile and applicable government requirements. Contact us to confirm availability for your business.
9. Which businesses need e-Invoice generation? +
As per government GST rules, businesses above a specified annual turnover threshold are required to generate e-Invoices for B2B transactions. Eligibility and thresholds are determined by government notification. We recommend consulting your CA or tax advisor for your specific situation.
10. Is thermal printing supported for POS? +
Yes, we support thermal receipts (80mm/58mm) out-of-the-box. Standard desktop laser printers are also supported for regular A4/A5 billing formats. Dot matrix printers are also supported for traditional counter billing.
11. Does the software generate GST reports? +
Yes. Create tax registers, input credit reports, and export compliance-friendly GSTR-1 & tax summaries. These reports support your return filing process.
12. Does Techstar support automated stock warnings? +
Yes. Easily configure low-stock indicators per warehouse/location. You'll receive alert notifications when items drop below threshold limits.
13. Can we import previous inventory records? +
Yes, you can import item masters, categorizations, SKUs, and purchase list entries via Excel templates.
14. Is my financial database secure? +
Techstar has built-in database firewalls, isolated workspaces, custom authorization logs, and regular offsite backups. Your data is accessible only to authorized users.
15. Do you provide setup training? +
Yes! Every trial account includes full screen-sharing setup training. Enterprise groups receive an account coordinator to map active outlets.
